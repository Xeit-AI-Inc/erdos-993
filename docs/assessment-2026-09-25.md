At eligible lower-half ranks C[j+1]<C[j]. The margin controls relative decay but the aggregate contains a positive binomial slope. In exact notation A=sum r_i e_i T_i[j], b=e0+sum r_i e_i and delta=q-j, one obtains S<=b Delta_j L^N-A/delta. Thus A>=b delta Delta_j L^N is a sufficient absolute-mass theorem. The weaker exact-ratio payment A[delta-(delta-1)C[j+1]/C[j]]>=b delta Delta_j L^N also suffices. Actual descent supplies an intermediate discount. These are alternative sufficient targets, not claims that MASS is necessary for a proof. A successful continuation should decide which target has a provable uniform mechanism, rather than simply accumulate equivalent formulas.
