natural `n`, including zero, the exact equality is

```text
(lam + lam^-1) * (a(n+1) * lam^(n+1) / Z)
  - a(n) * lam^n / Z - a(n+2) * lam^(n+2) / Z
= lam^n / Z * ((a(n+1)-a(n)) + lam^2 * (a(n+1)-a(n+2))).
```

## Retained Mathematics And Corrections

The synthesis retains, at **informal** grade, the full-configuration
conditional-binomial law. For independent `B`, put `C=V\B`; condition on a full
independent configuration `J` in `C`, and let `Y=|J|`, `M=|B\N(J)|`. Then

```text
Z(x) = sum_J x^Y (1+x)^M,
w(J) = lam^Y (1+lam)^M / Z(lam),
K | J = Y + Bin(M, lam/(1+lam)).
```

The joint configuration weights matter. Separate marginals of `Y` and `M`, or
an arbitrary nonforest mixture, cannot replace this forest law. Root-absent and
root-present partition functions retain exact child joins; means, variance and
the between-configuration covariance terms do not by themselves prove a sign.
Plateau-safe NoRecovery/weak-peak equivalence and centering calculus also survive
informally, not as additional Lean awards.

Two concrete informal obstructions sharpen future state design:

- On `P6` at activity 1, an occupation-maximizing set `{1,4,6}` need not remain
  occupation-maximizing after conditioning on vertex 1 being occupied. Its child
  restriction scores `5/8`, while `{3,6}` scores `3/4`. This rejects automatic
  inheritance for every maximizer, not every possible tie-break.
- The same `P4` unmarked polynomial `1+4x+3x^2` has different root-marked pairs.
  Attaching a parent produces `1+5x+6x^2+x^3` at one rooting and
  `1+5x+6x^2+2x^3` at another. Unmarked size law alone cannot determine the join;
  marked-pair and richer states remain viable.

Adjudication corrected three material errors that must not become inherited
