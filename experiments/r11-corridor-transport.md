## The Run's Central Finding

Both ingredient conjectures — (A) a favorable support above the crossing
boundary forces a negative whole-vertex deletion effect; (B) leaf deletion
drops the crossing index by at most one — reduce, by kernel-checked
equivalences, to residuals that are **one missing tool**: a comparison
between a tree's Delta-sequence and that of a derived forest at ranks
positioned by the parent's crossing index. Nothing in the governed corpus
makes that comparison in either direction. The run turned an inoperable
transport lemma (two of its three disjuncts' defining phrases were found
never to have been operationalized in the prior program's own sealed
corpus; the third is cut against by exhaustive measurement) into one
named missing lemma plus two governed reductions.
