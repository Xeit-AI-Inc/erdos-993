Set \(p=\alpha(T)-2\) **as an integer**, where \(\alpha\) is the independence number, and let \(x(T)\) be the first index with \(\Delta_j(T)<0\). Assume \(x(T)+2\le p\). For every *original* degree-one vertex \(v\), let \(s_v\) be its original support, \(H_v=T-\{v,s_v\}\), and \(R_v=T-N_T[s_v]\), with the original closed neighborhood. The strict original-rank selector is \(F_p(T)=\{v:\Delta_p(T-v)<0\}\). The theorem is

\[
S(T,p)=\sum_{v\in F_p(T)}\bigl(\Delta_{p-1}(H_v)-\Delta_{p-1}(R_v)\bigr)\le 0.
\]

Every selected original leaf contributes separately, including leaves sharing one support; an empty selector has sum zero. There is no residuality hypothesis. This is the first interior rank \(p=\alpha-2\), not the previously verified top-rank residual statement.
