## Cycle Results

### Cycle 4: multibase decomposition

Global signed neighborhood slack was decomposed into active-base slice slack
minus cross-base collision, with an exact full-fibre boundary correction. The
Lean theorem is generic finite typed-relation algebra; it does not prove Hall.

### Cycle 5: pure-deletion monotonicity

Cross-base collision is monotone under source deletion when the carriers,
base-label map, target set, and relation are fixed. Retagging and carrier
change are outside the theorem.

### Cycle 6: pair-collision capacity

The unordered distinct-base pair-overlap count double-counts as
`sum_y choose(d_y,2)` and bounds `sum_y pred(d_y)`. The required
formation-specific payment from that capacity remains open.

### Cycle 7: minimal deficient cores

Every nonempty inclusion-minimal deficient cut in a finite fixed relation has
deficit one, and deleting any one source leaves its neighborhood unchanged.
This strengthens the parent normal form but does not show that deficient cuts
cannot exist.

### Cycle 8: tree-shape-only obstruction

For every finite nontrivial tree, the endpoint-incidence relation has a full
vertex-side inclusion-minimal deficit-one cut while every proper cut satisfies
Hall and the incidence graph is itself a tree. Thus finiteness,
connectedness, acyclicity, deficit one, and absence of private neighbors do not
alone contradict deficiency. This is an abstract obstruction, not a governed
Delete/Retag counterexample.

### Cycle 9: signed-payment obstruction

The signed subset identity and four-fibre fixed-weight uniqueness theorem
forces weights `+1` on pairs, `-1` on triples, and `+1` on the four-set.
Therefore the unaugmented all-subcut representation cannot itself be a
termwise nonnegative Hall payment. Correction terms, global cancellation, or
additional governed structure are required.
