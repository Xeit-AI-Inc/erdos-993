Also `I(T-v)=I(H)+z I(R)`, so the selector is exactly `Delta_p(H)+Delta_(p-1)(R)<0`; this recurrence does not alter the pointwise argument or its rank.

Let `a=alpha(H)` and `k=p-1`. Every independent set of H extends by `v`, hence `a<=alpha(T)-1`. Eligibility gives `p>=2`, so `k>=1`. If `k>a`, both tagged counts vanish.
