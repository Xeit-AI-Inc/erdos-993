## Claim-by-claim audit

**C1-T2-ALG-IDENTITY — proposed_retained.** Fix an original leaf `v` and its original support `s`. The sets of `T-v` avoiding `s` are exactly those in `H=T-{v,s}`; the sets containing `s` are `s` plus a set in `R=T-N[s]`. Hence `I_(T-v)=I_H+z I_R`. Also the sets of `T` containing `v` are `v` plus a set in `H`, giving `I_T=(1+z)I_H+z I_R`. Since `R` is induced in `H`, the top degree of `I_T` is `alpha(H)+1`: the `(1+z)I_H` term supplies a positive top coefficient and no term has higher degree. Write `h_j=i_j(H)`, `r_j=i_j(R)` and use zero extension for every integer `j`. Then `a=Delta_p(T-v)=h_(p+1)-h_p+r_p-r_(p-1)` and `g=Delta_(p-1)(H)-Delta_(p-1)(R)=h_p-h_(p-1)-r_p+r_(p-1)`. Thus `g+a=h_(p+1)-h_(p-1)`. At `p=alpha(T)-2`, `g=h_(alpha-1)-h_(alpha-3)+|a|` whenever `a<0`. This direct proof validates the incoming derivation in [C1-T2/REPORT.md](cycles/cycle-1/C1-T2/REPORT.md) without numerical evidence or eligibility.

**C1-T2-ALPHA2-BUDGET — proposed_retained.** Rearrangement gives `g<=0` for a favorable leaf if and only if `i_(alpha-3)(H)-i_(alpha-1)(H)>=-Delta_(alpha-2)(T-v)`. The inequality for every favorable leaf would therefore imply the complete sum is nonpositive, including multiplicity at shared supports. No step in the incoming report establishes the inequality from `x(T)+2<=alpha(T)-2`; no conclusion about the primary claim follows. An aggregate inequality could still hold when a pointwise inequality fails. The inherited T22 marked leaf has positive `g` at rank 34 while `alpha=68` and `x=32`, so eligibility alone does not give pointwise sign at arbitrary eligible ranks; it is not a counterexample at the required rank 66. See [t22-order91.json](sources/standing-controls/t22-order91.json) and the semantic distinction in [SEMANTIC-CONTRACT.md](SEMANTIC-CONTRACT.md).

## Directed falsification and scope

It checks `T_m` for `m=8,22,40` and 30 sequential uniform Prüfer draws at each order `12,18,24,30` from one RNG seed `582221`, for 123 trees total. Exactly 94 satisfy the contract's eligibility. No eligible case had a positive favorable summand or positive complete sum. In particular the inherited `T22` has `alpha=68`, `x=32`, `p=66`, 67 favorable leaves, maximum favorable `g=-2079`, and complete `S=-147807` at the target rank, whereas its cited positive marked summand is at rank 34. This targeted check tests the most obvious inherited pointwise obstruction at the actual interior rank. It neither proves the rank-budget inequality nor establishes a finite order cutoff. The output includes all 123 rows in `targeted-probe.json` and uses exact integer tree DP from the copied pinned evaluator.

The inherited universal tree log-concavity obstruction, fixed/tag-closed Hall cuts, raw edge-difference and marginal-extraction failures do not enter the elementary deletion identity.
