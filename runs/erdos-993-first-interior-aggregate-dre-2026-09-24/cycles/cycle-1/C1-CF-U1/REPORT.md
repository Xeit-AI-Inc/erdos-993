## Dispositions

- **C1-U1-SUPPORT-FIBRE-POLYNOMIAL-IDENTITY — proposed_retained (informal identity).** Let `s` support `lambda_s >= 1` original leaves and fix a tagged leaf `v` there. In `T-{v,s}`, the other `lambda_s-1` leaves are isolated. Because `T` is a tree, each non-leaf neighbor `u` of `s` heads a separate component `C_{s,u}`. This yields `I(H_v)=(1+z)^(lambda_s-1) product I(C_{s,u})=H_s`. Deleting the original closed neighborhood of `s` deletes every leaf there and every branch root, yielding `I(R_v)=product I(C_{s,u}-u)=R_s`. Independent sets of `T-v` that omit `s` are counted by `H_s`; sets that contain `s` contribute `zR_s`. Thus `I(T-v)=H_s+zR_s` coefficientwise. Taking the rank-`p` difference gives the displayed strict selector; subtracting the `H_s` and `R_s` rank-`p-1` differences gives `[z^p]Q_s-[z^(p-1)]Q_s`. Each tagged leaf at `s` has the same selector and summand, so its support contributes `lambda_s` copies, never one. Integer negative and out-of-degree indices are zero by `SEMANTIC-CONTRACT.md`. Empty branch products equal one, covering stars and the two-vertex tree; a one-vertex tree has no original leaves and no supports, so the grouped identity is an empty sum. No residual assumption or rank substitution is used. This is an exact informal counting proof, not a Lean award. The general deletion recurrence is routine; the origin claims no novelty. A registered degree-two special-case coefficient identity exists in `sources/master/CLAIMS-COMPACT.json` under `E993-ORDINARY-DEG2-SIBLING-G1-COEFFICIENT-IDENTIFICATION`, so the present packaging must not be advertised as a new theorem without identity review.
