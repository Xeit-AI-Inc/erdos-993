## Instrument

- **Tree generation, no networkx.** Free (unlabelled) trees of order `n` are
  generated from the order-`(n-1)` representatives by attaching a new leaf to
  every vertex of every `(n-1)`-order representative, then deduplicating by
  an AHU canonical form: tree centre(s) found by iterated leaf-peeling
  (1 or 2 vertices), rooted canonical string computed at each centre
  (post-order, children's strings sorted, wrapped in parentheses), and for
  two centres the free-tree invariant is the lexicographic minimum of the two
  full-tree rooted strings. This closure is complete because every order-`n`
  tree yields, by deleting any one of its leaves, an order-`(n-1)` tree to
  which it can be re-attached at that leaf's former neighbour.
  `generate_trees_upto(17)` reproduces **A000055 exactly**,
  `1,1,1,2,3,6,11,23,47,106,235,551,1301,3159,7741,19320,48629` for orders

  occurred).

  `n-1` edges **and** a single DFS-reachable component from vertex 0; called

  representative list, and again before every polynomial computation.
