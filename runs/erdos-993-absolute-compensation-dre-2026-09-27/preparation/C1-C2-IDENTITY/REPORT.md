## New restricted MASS identity

Propose `E993-PATH-STAR-ARITY-2-4-M266-SELECTED-LOWER-MASS`. For the contract's ordinary path-star with `m>=266`, distinct arity-2/3/4 branches, integer-zero-extended coefficients, and actual first strict parent descent `x`, take **each** natural `p` satisfying all three guards `x+2<=p`, `3p<2(N+2)+1`, and `2p<=N+2`. Put `j=p-2`, `delta=N+1-j`, `D_j=binom(N,j+1)-binom(N,j)`, `e0=1[Delta_p A0<0]`, `ei=1[Delta_p Ai<0]`, `b=e0+sum_i r_i ei`, and `A=sum_i r_i ei T_i[j]`, with `A0=LQ+zL^N`, `Ai=G B_(r_i-1)H_i+zL^N`, `C=GQ`, `T_i=GF_(r_i)H_i`. The proposed statement is **`A>=b delta D_j`**. The endpoint counts once; branch `i` counts its `r_i` original private-tip tags; the strict flags are recomputed at the current `p`. Empty selection is included. This is a proper cutoff restriction of registered OPEN MASS key 455, and a stronger claim than registered VERIFIED `m>=266` aggregate key 450. The aggregate sign cannot imply MASS, so the cutoff MASS has its own semantic identity.

Proposed grade: **source-dependent informal analytic proof**, with exact-integer scalar subcertificate, no Lean award. C1-AF's independent audit uses the predecessor's intermediate `5x>2N-1` rank bound and perturbed deletion/parent mixed-minor and deletion log-concavity estimates to obtain full strict selection at the actual eligible lower-half `p`. The stated monotonicity propagates it to all `m>=266`, `2m<=N<=4m` and the needed rank band. ## New refuted spread quotient identity

Propose `E993-PATH-STAR-ARITY-2-4-SPREAD-NORMALIZED-EXACT-RATIO-NONDECREASING`, with **proposed REFUTED** status at exact literal-witness grade. For every fixed-`(m,N)` replacement of two arity-3 branches by arities 2 and 4, and every natural `p` satisfying the contract's three lower-half guards for **both** profiles, compute each profile's own first descent and actual current-`p` strict flags. Where both selected-tag counts `b_old,b_new` are positive, define its normalized exact-ratio quotient

`Q = (delta C[j]-(delta-1)C[j+1]) A / (b delta D_j C[j])`.

Here `j=p-2` and `delta,D_j` are common to the fixed-`N` pair, while `C,A,b` are profile-specific. The proposed universal assertion `Q_new>=Q_old` fails at `(a2,a3,a4)=(0,10,13)->(1,8,14)`, `m=23,N=82,p=42`, with both first descents `x=40` and every represented endpoint/tip flag strictly on. C1-AU's literal-tree replay checks the polynomial formulas and all earlier parent differences. Both counts are `b=83`; both exact payment margins are **positive**, but the exact cross product for `Q_new-Q_old` is negative. Independently recalculating from the C1-AU table reproduces the two positive margins and the negative quotient comparison. The counterexample refutes *nondecrease of this quotient only*: it is not a failure of the primary payment, does not prove nonincrease or threshold preservation, and does not decide registered OPEN spread-`S` monotonicity key 454 or spread first-descent key 456.
