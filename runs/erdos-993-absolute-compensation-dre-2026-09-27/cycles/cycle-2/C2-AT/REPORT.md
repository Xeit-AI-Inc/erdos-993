## Universal arguments and decisive correction

**Occupancy bounds (T1 and the corresponding T3 coefficient claim).** For a uniform `u`-subset of disjoint blocks of total size `M`, expand `prod_i(1+X_i/r_i)` over block subsets `I`. The event that every block in `I` contains exactly one selected element contributes `(prod_{i in I}r_i) binom(M-sum_{i in I}r_i,u-|I|)/binom(M,u)`; the product of reciprocal arities cancels. Summing gives `Q[u]/binom(M,u)` exactly, without independence. Finite Jensen applied to `sum_i X_i log(1+1/r_i)` gives the source's rational-power inequality, with marginal `Pr(X_i=1)=r_i binom(M-r_i,u-1)/binom(M,u)`. Since `log(1+1/r)>=2/(2r+1)` and `exp(v)>=sum_{h=0}^d v^h/h!` for `v>=0`, the stated rational `H_i[u]` lower bound follows at every in-range `u`, and is zero-extended otherwise. Convolution with the nonnegative `GF_2=(1,2)`, `GF_3=(2,5,2)`, `GF_4=(3,9,7,2)` gives the `T_i[j]` lower bounds. The empty cofactor (`M=0`) is covered. This is an informal universal proof of the coefficient bounds, not a selected payment proof.

**Selector threshold (T2).** The polynomial identity `A0-Ai=z^3 F_(r_i)H_i` yields `Delta_p Ai=u-d_i`, where `u=Delta_p A0` and `d_i=Delta_(p-3)(F_(r_i)H_i)`. Thus `e0=1 iff u<0` and `ei=1 iff d_i>u`. Equality turns the strict branch flag off. Endpoint-only selection is exactly `u<0` with all represented `d_i<=u`. If it occurs in an eligible row, its payment margin is `-delta D_j C[j]<0`, because `A=0,b=1` and `D_j,delta,C[j]>0`. This conditional obstruction gives no independent universal endpoint-exclusion theorem.

**First descent (T3).** Put `R_f(k)=(k+1)f[k+1]/((deg f-k)f[k])`. The product rule expresses `R_(ab)(j)` as a weighted average of `R_a(u),R_b(v)` with nonnegative weights `(deg a-u)a[u]b[v]` and `(deg b-v)a[u]b[v]` over `u+v=j`. For `G`, this ratio is 2; for `B_r`, it is `(r+1)/r` at zero, `r/(r+1)` at one, and 1 later. All are at least `2/3`. Hence `C[j+1]/C[j]>=2(N+1-j)/(3(j+1))>=1` when `5j<=2N-1`. The binomial parent term rises strictly there, so the *actual first* descent obeys `5x>2N-1` for all `m>=1`. At an eligible row, `j>=x` and `beta_x=binom(q,x)-binom(q,x-1)>0`; descent gives `C[x+1]/C[x]<1-v`, `v=beta_x/C[x]`. Log-concavity of the factors and convolution gives `t=C[j+1]/C[j]<=C[x+1]/C[x]`. Therefore the **correct** exact-ratio factor satisfies

`kappa=1-t+t/delta=1-(1-1/delta)t > v+(1-v)/delta`.

The T3 source and C2-CU-T3 instead write `kappa=1-t+1/delta` and assert `kappa>v+1/delta`. That normalization is false. Consequently the source's displayed scalar test with `v+1/delta` has no demonstrated implication. The repaired sufficient test is: `b=0`, or `(v+(1-v)/delta) sum_{i:ei=1} r_i ell_i[j] >= bD_j`, where `ell_i` is the rational occupancy lower bound. Indeed `A>=sum r_i ell_i[j]>=0` and the strict bound on `kappa` imply `kappa A>=bD_j`. No route proves this repaired scalar premise on every actual eligible selected row; in particular it cannot pay an endpoint-only row.
