## Exact predicate and non-implications

For `m>=1` distinct centers of arities `r_i in {2,3,4}`, put `N=sum_i r_i`, `q=N+1`, `alpha=N+2`, `L=1+z`, `G=1+2z`, `B_r=L^r+z`, `F_r=sum_(h=0)^(r-2)L^h`, `Q=prod_i B_(r_i)`, `H_i=prod_(h!=i)B_(r_h)`, `C=GQ`, `T_i=G F_(r_i)H_i`, and `P=C+zL^q`. Coefficients and binomials are integer zero-extended. The parent descent `x` is the **least natural** `k` for which `Delta_k P=P[k+1]-P[k]<0`, including the terminal difference. For every natural `p` satisfying all three guards `x+2<=p`, `3p<2alpha+1`, and `2p<=alpha`, let `j=p-2`, `delta=q-j`, `D_j=binom(N,j+1)-binom(N,j)`. The actual-current-`p` strict selectors are `e0=1[Delta_p A0<0]` and `ei=1[Delta_p Ai<0]`, with `A0=LQ+zL^N`, `Ai=G B_(r_i-1)H_i+zL^N`. The one endpoint tag and `r_i` original private-tip tags per center give `b=e0+sum_i r_i ei` and `A=sum_i r_i ei T_i[j]`.

The integer target is

`M := (delta*C[j]-(delta-1)*C[j+1])*A - b*delta*D_j*C[j] >= 0`.

At an eligible row, `C[j]>0`, `delta>0`, `D_j>0`, and `0<t=C[j+1]/C[j]<1`. Thus, with `kappa=1-t+t/delta`, `M>=0` is exactly `kappa A>=bD_j`. The normalized factor **is not** `1-t+1/delta`; that display in T2 was an algebraic typo. The raw integer grid tests used the correct `M`. Since `kappa>=1/delta`, selected MASS `A>=b delta D_j` implies payment, but payment need not imply MASS. The occupation condition `(1-t)A>=bD_j` is stronger than the exact-ratio sufficient condition. Empty selection makes `A=b=M=0` and is included. Endpoint-only selection would make `M<0`; excluding it structurally remains an obligation for any route relying on that exclusion.

The formally verified relative margin says `T_i[j+1]<=((delta-1)/delta)t T_i[j]`. Multiplying by the nonnegative **current** weights `r_i ei` yields `S<=bD_j-kappa A`, so payment implies the selected aggregate sign `S<=0`. The converse fails as a deduction: put `epsilon_i=((delta-1)/delta)t T_i[j]-T_i[j+1]>=0` and `R=sum_i r_i ei epsilon_i`. Direct subtraction gives the exact identity `S=bD_j-kappa A-R`. Thus the critic's `R>=(bD_j-kappa A)_+` is equivalent to `S<=0`, an algebraic restatement of the accepted aggregate, not a proof of `M>=0`. This is the distinction between the registered primary, the stronger separate MASS claim, and the already verified aggregate.
