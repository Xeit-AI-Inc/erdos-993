# Weighted marked-tip recurrence: a direct algebraic corollary

This is a controller preparation note, not a new registered theorem or an assertion of a sign. It follows by the positive original-weight sum from the already reviewed marked-tip identity. Retain the exact ordinary arity2/3/4 path-star notation of the C5 handoff. Put V_i=G H_i, Z=sum_i r_i V_i, and W=sum_i r_i A_i. Since B_r=L B_(r-1)-z^2 and P=L A_i-z^2 V_i, summing with the ORIGINAL positive multiplicities gives

    L W = N P + z^2 Z.

For zero-extended coefficients, at every integer k this yields

    W[k]+W[k-1] = N P[k]+Z[k-2],
    Delta_k W+Delta_(k-1) W = N Delta_k P+Delta_(k-2) Z.

Thus a direct selector route could try to control the cofactor sum Z and the two adjacent W differences. Even if the right side is negative, that alone only forces at least one of the two W differences to be negative. The required selector is at the exact current p, so an alternating recurrence, boundary value, or additional shape lemma is necessary. Do not silently replace current-p strict descent by descent at p-1. Likewise, positivity of the coefficients in Z supplies no required sign of its forward difference. The already known individual shifted comparison implies the weighted one by a common-C sum; this recurrence offers a different algebraic lens without assuming that comparison.

For a marked tip this is precisely the reviewed P=L A_i-z^2 V_i relation, not new mathematical scope. No claim-identity entry or status promotion is warranted for merely writing its weighted sum. A successful new inequality must separately identify its guards, proof and bridge to current-p strict selection.
