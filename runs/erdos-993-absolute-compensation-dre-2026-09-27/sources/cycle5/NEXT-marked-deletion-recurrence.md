# Private marked-deletion recurrence and mixed minor

Controller algebraic lead for later sealed intake, not a current C4 input or new theorem award. For a specified tip of arity r, put H=product of all other branch factors and V=G H. Then U=B_(r-1)V, E=z L^N, A=U+E and C=B_r V. Since B_r=L B_(r-1)-z^2, the actual parent satisfies P=C+L E=L A-z^2 V. All terms refer to the original family and specified original leaf; there is no change of multiplicity or favorable-leaf definition.

The guarded shifted C/deletion comparison is the nonnegativity of
[U_k C_k-U_(k+1) C_(k-1)] + [E_k C_k-E_(k+1) C_(k-1)].
The second bracket can be negative inside the rank guard (C4-T1 exact witness); a structural proof needs a quantitative lower bound on the first bracket that pays for that deficit, not separate nonnegative summands. An explicit two-color switching map for the mixed minor, or an inequality bounding the perturbation relative to the main comparison, might supply this. The identity P=L A-z^2 V also gives a direct bridge between parent/deletion minors and the cofactor coefficients, but its negative term must be retained. No general sign inference follows from the identity alone.

Potential induction must account for boundary strips: adding a branch changes N and hence the allowed ranks 2k<=N+2. Convolution can import coefficients outside the old valid range. Do not assume closure of guarded or weakly synchronized inequalities. This is a proof plan to investigate, not a claimed solution.
