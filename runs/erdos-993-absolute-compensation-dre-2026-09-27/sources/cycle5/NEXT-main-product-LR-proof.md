# Private elementary main-product ratio proof

This is a controller derivation for later review; it does not close the full deletion comparison. Let V=G H_i, b be the coefficients of B_r, d those of B_(r-1), C=b*V and U=d*V. Extend d by zero to degree r. The local ratios d_t/b_t are nonincreasing: for r2 they are (1,2/3,0), for r3 (1,3/4,1/3,0), and for r4 (1,4/5,1/2,1/4,0). All b_t are positive on0..r. The cofactor V has positive interval support and log-concave coefficients because its factors G and B2,B3,B4 do.

For two consecutive total ranks k,k+1, the normalized weights on t proportional to b_t V[k-t] and b_t V[k+1-t] are ordered in likelihood ratio: their weight ratio is a constant times V[k+1-t]/V[k-t], nondecreasing in t by log-concavity of V. The cross-product formulation includes zero boundary weights; it follows directly from the Toeplitz2-minors of V and avoids dividing by zero. Thus the expectation of the nonincreasing d_t/b_t cannot increase, proving U[k+1]/C[k+1]<=U[k]/C[k] on the positive common support. At U's terminal boundary the same cross-multiplied inequality holds by zero extension.

Since C is log-concave, it follows that U[k+1] C[k-1]<=U[k] C[k] for interior k. This proves the main-product portion of the desired shifted comparison. The actual deletion polynomial is A=U+E, E=z L^N; adding E requires a quantitative surplus estimate. The E-only minor can be negative inside the lower-half guard, so this monotonicity statement cannot simply be added to an assumed E inequality.
