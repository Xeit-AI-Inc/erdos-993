# Private future-cycle symmetric-function lead

Li, Li, Yang and Zhang, [A symmetric function approach to log-concavity of independence polynomials](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.04245), inspected v1: Theorem1.3 expresses log-concavity through positivity of two-row Schur coefficients of the symmetric-function construction. The paper applies cancellation of negative terms to spiders and develops symmetric-function deletion recurrences. Its spider theorem concerns a tree with one vertex of degree at least3. Our path-star family generally has many such vertices, so that theorem does not directly apply. This is a method lead, not a new theorem import or a formal result.

Controller proposal: investigate whether a mixed determinant for parent/deletion or shifted-C/deletion coefficients admits a direct two-color independent-set switching interpretation. In a tree, the union of two disjoint color classes induces a bipartite forest; exchanging colors on a component changes rank imbalance. A marked-leaf condition and the original multiplicities must survive any injection. The arity2/3/4 restriction and the lower-half guard must enter explicitly; a generic forest-switching claim would collide with existing obstruction fences. Seek an actual sign-preserving map or exact uncancelled remainder, not a reformulation of the inequality in Schur notation. This is not a proof or registered claim.
