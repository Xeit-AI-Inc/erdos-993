# C3 Stage 1 claim identity review

## Decision

Before C3 dispatch, assign the three **OPEN** identities in [CANDIDATES.json](CANDIDATES.json): (1) the all-`m`, actual-eligible, every-branch bound `2T_i[j] >= 3 delta D_j`; (2) adjacent-arity balancing of the rational Jensen **exponent**; and (3) the profile-sensitive strict-descent rank bound. These are distinct universal predicates. The private C3 arguments and calculations have not been independently audited and establish none of them here. No registry or contract status is changed by this review.

The first predicate is branch-local, without a strict-selector premise. It is stronger in one direction than needed for selected MASS, but it does not handle endpoint-only selection. The second orders only the exponent in a proposed cofactor lower bound at fixed `(m',M,k)`; it does not order coefficients, payment quotients, selectors, or actual parent descents. The third constrains actual strict-descent ranks; it says nothing about a selector or payment sign. Their precise domains, inclusive boundaries, and aliases are in the JSON.

## Identity and terminal-clash audit

I scanned all 494 frozen registered claims, including each statement and every alias and alias pattern, for the proposed predicates and nearby terminology. None of the three has an existing canonical identity or exact alias. The registered selected MASS and exact-ratio payment are already separate **OPEN** targets; the registered all-`m` selected aggregate is **VERIFIED** at computer-assisted grade. The `m>=266` selected MASS restriction is already **VERIFIED**, but does not assert the every-branch local bound. The registered `m=42..265` lower-half base and `m>=266` analytic tail explicitly include full original-leaf selection on their stated ranges. The proposed direct prefix check covers `m=1..69` and therefore overlaps the `42..69` part; overlap is a check, not a new predicate.

No new key is needed for the `m>=120` local argument, the relaxed `m=70..119` balanced finite protocol, or the `m<=69` direct-factor protocol. They are proof subcases or bounded instruments for the new all-`m` branchwise predicate and the existing MASS/payment targets. The C2 occupancy identity, Jensen cofactor floor, corrected `GF_r` convolution, first-descent estimate `5x>2N-1`, and relative main-mark margin are tools, with their existing C2 scope and evidence grades. A census-free selector proof is a desired proof method for a literal selection/exclusion proposition already covered, at inherited grades, by the aggregate sign or by the registered selection clauses plus the finite prefix if validated. Do not create a fresh **OPEN** literal selector key merely because a stronger, independent proof is sought.

The nearby **REFUTED** key `E993-PATH-STAR-ARITY-2-4-SPREAD-NORMALIZED-EXACT-RATIO-NONDECREASING` forbids interpreting Jensen-exponent balancing as actual normalized-payment monotonicity under `(3,3)->(2,4)`. The **REFUTED** full-bracket degree-ULC and branch-append descent-step claims are likewise not revived by the local coefficient or profile-rank wording. The existing **OPEN** spread first-descent and shared-rank `S` monotonicity keys have different quantifiers. None is an alias of a proposed C3 key.

## Evidence boundary and possible later consequence

The private small-prefix JSON reports 59,639 count profiles, 68,129 actual eligible rows, and zero local, MASS, primary, endpoint-only, or non-full-selection failures. The private balanced finite JSON reports 799,895 relaxed scalar states for `m=70..119`, zero excluded states, and a minimum reported left/right ratio above 1.38. These are producer outputs, not an independent replay or a universal award. The `m>=120` analytic constants, exponent convexity, differential operator, feasibility of every `GF_r` shift, and exact evaluator implementations all await the 9/18/3/1 cycle's independent audit. A branch-local failure would refute only that new sufficient bound unless it also produces an exact selected MASS/payment failure.

If the three range components for the branch-local bound survive review, the all-`m` branchwise predicate could be supported at computer-assisted informal grade. A separate valid composition could then use the inherited `m=42..265` full-selection clause for `70..119` and `120..265`, the registered `m>=266` MASS tail, and independently replayed `m<=69` MASS checks. That chain would prove the exact existing MASS and payment predicates without taking the already-verified aggregate **sign** as a premise. Alternatively, the aggregate sign itself logically excludes endpoint-only selection because that case gives `S=D_j>0`; such a consequence inherits its evidence grade and is not an independent structural replacement proof. Distinguish predicate truth and registry evidence grade from the contract's governed-Lean decisive-stop requirement. A reviewed computer-assisted closure must not be labeled a Lean award or trigger an early stop.
