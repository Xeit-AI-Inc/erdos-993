# Controller-private possible directions after C3 review

These are questions, not proof claims or dispatch authority. Use only if C3 synthesis leaves an independent census-free selector argument central.

1. Compare a private-tip deletion Ai directly with endpoint deletion A0, rather than proving every Ai/P likelihood-ratio inequality. The desired implication is only Delta_p A0<0 => at least one Delta_p Ai<0 at actual eligible ranks. A candidate may choose a branch of maximal arity. Pure arity2 has no eligible LOWER-half ranks (inherited x=m+1, while p>=m+3 and p<=m+1), so a nonempty target row has at least one branch of arity3 or4. This may allow a one-branch argument without full selector saturation.

2. Exact leaf recurrence identities: P=L Ai-z^2 GHi and P=L A0-z^2 Q. Also A0-Ai=z^3 F_r Hi. These are ordinary polynomial identities, not selector implications by themselves. For main terms before the common binomial perturbation, the local pairs (G B_(r-1), L B_r) have coefficient ratios nonincreasing forr2,3,4; convolving by the LC Hi preserves adjacent LR minors. The equal added perturbation zL^N still needs a separate argument; adding the same positive sequence does not automatically preserve LR order.

3. Quantitative first-descent curvature: use BOTH consecutive parent inequalities, not just Delta_x C<-beta_x. A sufficiently controlled upper bound on the local curvature of C could turn first-descent timing into an absolute coefficient bound or a deletion-slope comparison. Bounded-degree independent factors suggest a size-dependent curvature estimate, but no such estimate has been proved here. Do not replace ordinary LC by unproved ultra-log-concavity.

4. Exact complement identity B_r-G=zL F_r gives C-G^2Hi=zL Ti. The derivative identity z sum_i r_i Ti=C'-2Q-G sum_i(r_i+1)Hi is also exact. These could relate marked mass to a parent slope, but neither is a positivity theorem or a selector proof by itself. Current local coefficient bounds may make further mass work redundant; prioritize the independent selector gap if that is what survives C3.
