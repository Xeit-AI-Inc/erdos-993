# C5 controller simplification probe: cross terms need not be nonnegative

Unawarded exact computation, available for later-cycle guidance. With the root candidate split Z=a+b, P=V+d, define LCcross_j=2a_j b_j-a_(j-1)b_(j+1)-b_(j-1)a_(j+1), and LRcross_j=a_j d_(j+1)+b_j V_(j+1)-a_(j+1)d_j-b_(j+1)V_j. For A0/Astar use b=zL^(3m); for R use b=(3m+1)L^(3m); d=z(1+z)L^(3m). Obtain main components by exact subtraction from the already pinned polynomial identities.

Across m3..100,j=m..2m+2 (16023 Z/rank checks), every LCcross is nonnegative, as are A0/Astar LRcross. But R LRcross is negative on895 rows, first m79,j116; the exact negative integer is in C5-CROSS-PERTURBATION-SIGN-DIAGNOSTIC.json. Extending selected sizes reveals LCcross negative at m400, including A0 j607, Astar j602, R j606; selected m600,800,1000,2000 also have negative cross terms. See C5-CROSS-LC-LARGE-M-DIAGNOSTIC.json. These do not refute LC or mixed minors of the full sums, which include positive main terms. They block the tempting simplification of assuming all cross terms nonnegative on the central range merely from modest samples. Perturbation-margin reasoning still applies.
