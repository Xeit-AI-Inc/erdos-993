# Root quantitative coefficient direction, unawarded

For b_(m,j)=[z^j](1+4z+3z^2+z^3)^m, differentiation gives the exact recurrence
(j+1)b_(m,j+1)=4(m-j)b_(m,j)+3(2m-j+1)b_(m,j-1)+(3m-j+2)b_(m,j-2), with integer zero extension.
This may support a verified ratio barrier or a holonomic inequality certificate. The first coefficient on the right becomes negative in the important central range, so positivity of the recurrence is not automatic.

Possible all-m T_m route: quantitative adjacent-ratio decay of the log-concave main convolution component, combined with a uniform pointwise coefficient bound for the binomial perturbation and an explicit finite cutoff. Total mass alone is insufficient. Main and perturbation mean separation grows like m/18; it cannot be treated as adjacent modes. All orbit selectors must be separately proved.

Bounded fresh literature search found Saumard-Wellner arXiv1404.5886 and Wellner 2013 Strong Log-concavity is Preserved by Convolution (doi10.1007/978-3-0348-0490-5_7). Their retrieved abstracts distinguish continuous strong log-concavity and discrete ultra-log-concavity. They do NOT, on this retrieval, provide the needed discrete curvature bound for B. B is not ultra-log-concave in the normalized finite-support sense, so no ULC preservation theorem may be imported without verifying another applicable hypothesis.
