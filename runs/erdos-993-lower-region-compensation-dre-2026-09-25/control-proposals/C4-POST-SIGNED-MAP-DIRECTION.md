# After the signed-map obstruction

Unawarded controller successor note. The C4-T4 signed deletion rule is structurally susceptible to boundary-of-boundary cancellation. Retagging based only on the final face preserves that cancellation. Replacing it by homology or quotienting out boundaries does not by itself recover the original dimension inequality; missing kernel dimensions would need accounting.

Potential alternative: characteristic-zero unsigned incidence/Lefschetz-type maps in a commutative square-zero algebra, rather than a simplicial differential. The star counterexample does not refute that different operator; local unsigned injectivity is separately refuted on the selected T22 arm, so actual cross-tag transfer is essential. Generic independent edge weights would convert full rank to structural Hall, returning to a known OPEN mechanism rather than producing a free proof. A new algebraic route must specify a realizable cross-tag operator, handle selectors and original supports, and prove a non-tautological rank property. Do not treat the exact C4 signed refutation as a refutation of all algebraic or topological approaches, and do not restart the same boundary under renamed notation.
