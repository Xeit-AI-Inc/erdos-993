# C5 preparatory intake review

**Disposition: conditional approval of the six-axis research allocation, with packet corrections required before dispatch.** This is a bounded source and design review, not a C5 claim award or a review of the final C4 synthesis bytes. I read only the twelve files hashed in `RESULTS.json`; I did not run the builder, dispatch workers, build Lean, or inspect other worker outputs.

## Allocation and mathematical framing

The draft has exactly six T, six F, and six U routes. T1/F1/U1 address the uniform `T_m` certificate; the five remaining triples cover bounded heterogeneous branches, selected matching-slack charging, strict-selector propagation, unsigned cross-tag maps, and corrected shell work. T6/F6/U6 are the three shell-focused seats. The proposed 18 Luna-high searches, 36 cross-orientation Luna-high critics, three Sol-high origin adjudicators, and one Sol-high synthesis match `WORKER-PROTOCOL.md`, as does the three-seat ceiling. The route text asks for independent proofs, attacks, explicit gaps, complete selectors, and exact original-leaf sums. It does not self-award the `T_m` family or the arbitrary-tree primary.

The candidate family is **exactly** the existing `E993-ORDINARY-TM-LOWER-REGION-AGGREGATE` identity named in the independent audit; it is a theorem for the specified ordinary trees `T_m`, all `m>=1`, under the original strict lower-region selector and guards. It is not the general `E993-LOWER-REGION-ORDINARY-FAVORABLE-LEAF-AGGREGATE`. The preparatory audit calls its proof computer-assisted and reports 1,507,464 positive LC margins, the same number of mixed margins, and 276,938 eligible rows, with no failures. These are exact Python replay data, not a Lean kernel check. The Lean-readiness report explicitly leaves graph/count fidelity, a formal finite-order ULC convolution theorem, the R27 theorem binding, the mixed-minor algebra, and a kernel-checked `m=3..999` finite base to a future formal project. In particular, `B=1+4z+3z^2+z^3` must be used at ULC order 4, not order 3. The audited Gurvits/Liggett finite-order convolution result is an imported primary theorem whose hypotheses and bridge must remain visible to routes; the existing R27 forest-descent bound `|T|<=4x` is a separate registered dependency. None of this promotes `T_m` at intake.

The direction audit also fences the useful matching-slack identity from the still-missing **selected weighted** lower bound. T3/U3 should be judged on a new class and a real budget, not a rearrangement of addability. The C4 fence report supplies two separate proposed refutations: the specified signed face-only retagging map and local nonpositive marked/addability covariance. Their negative witnesses leave the full ordinary aggregate open. The allocation respects those scopes.

## Corrections required in the final common packet

1. **Restore directly pinned inherited sources.** The draft builder's `fixed_sources()` includes only 16 of the 179 files in `packets/C4-COMMON.json:allowed_source_files`; 163 are absent from the generated C5 direct allowed list. A hash-pinned C4 common packet used as a provenance pointer does not grant workers direct access under the exact-source rule. The omissions include `inputs/ordinary_tree_checked.py` (the contract/protocol evaluator), `inputs/C4-TRANSITIVE-REFUTED-CLAIMS.json`, the R27 theorem contract and verification artifacts, C3/C4 first-shell award artifacts, `inputs/standing-controls/*`, and CB/orbit/shell instrument protocols, `run.py` files and result files needed for graph recipes and replay. The current evaluator erratum named by the contract/protocol also needs an explicit C5 pin. Carry these as historical/verified/proposal grades according to their actual source status; a current snapshot overrides only claim-status authority, not historical evidence scope. The final source list may include additional C4 material, but every actual worker read needs its own pin.
2. **Include the audited C4 fence as a graded proposal.** The builder includes the signed-map audit but none of `preparation/C4-PREP-FINAL-FENCE-IDENTITIES/{REPORT.md,CLAIMS-DRAFT.json,REPLAY.py,EVIDENCE.json}`. Those exact refutation statements and witnesses should be visible when the current approved snapshot registers them; their scratchpad report by itself has no authority. If any proposed identity is installed after C4 closeout, the current identity and ledger must agree on its exact status and scope.
3. **Use a compact neutral read-first set.** The draft `read_first` demands both entire identity and ledger plus allocation, while the protocol requires a neutral handoff. A concise C5 handoff/authority summary, contract, protocol, and allocation can be read first; keep the full current snapshots hash-pinned for exact-identity lookup. The historical `C4-NEUTRAL-HANDOFF.md` should be marked historical, not passed as the current neutral C5 handoff. Avoid calling proposals or worker syntheses awarded results in that summary.
4. **Audit the chosen current snapshot against closeout.** `check_snapshot()` proves that identity and ledger have matching key/status maps and that the primary and `T_m` remain `OPEN`. It does not establish that the copied snapshot is the final post-C4-closeout authority, that claim statements are unchanged, or that separately approved new refutations have been incorporated. The closeout guard only checks for a `C4`/`Cycle 4` byte string. Controller reconciliation is therefore a required external admission step. Preserve `T_m=OPEN` at intake; C4 portfolio and synthesis returns remain `proposed_worker_only` and are not allowed to promote a claim.

The builder's top-level C4-AT, C4-AF, C4-AU, and C4-SYNTHESIS directory collection is consistent with the protocol's self-contained adjudication/synthesis handoff, subject to final admission and hash review. The script creates a fresh scratch child with exclusive files and no dispatch. Its report explicitly leaves final paths to the controller; I see no reason to wait for the final C4 synthesis bytes before making the above packet-design corrections.

## Boundary of this review

I did not verify the source files that the builder proposes to pin, replay the large finite base, independently prove the all-`m` algebra, validate a final post-closeout identity/ledger, or inspect C4 worker content. Conditional approval applies to the allocation and packet shape after the four corrections above, not to a mathematical status change or an actual C5 dispatch.
