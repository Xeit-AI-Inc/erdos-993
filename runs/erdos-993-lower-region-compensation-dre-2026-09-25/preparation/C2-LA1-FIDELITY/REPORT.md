## Independent semantic check

`IsGraphLeaf T v` means exactly one original neighbor. Under that guard, `support T v` is that neighbor; its unspecified off-leaf value is immaterial. `H T v` is the original two-vertex deletion set `{v,s}`. `R T v` inserts `s` into the original graph's `neighborFinset s`, hence deletes the original closed neighborhood `N_T[s]`. `indepSetsAvoiding` counts independent subsets of the undeleted vertices using original adjacency. Its `powersetCard` gives zero at unsupported natural ranks. `forwardDifferenceDel` casts those natural counts to `Int` before subtraction, so no truncated natural subtraction occurs.

`IsolateCovered` says every whole-graph isolate belongs to `W`. `taggedFamily` filters independent sets of exact rank by non-disjointness from `W`, so `markedCount` counts each marked independent set once, including the correct zero at rank zero and out-of-range ranks. The first conjunct universally quantifies arbitrary finite simple graphs, marks and natural `k`; its only substantive guards are isolate coverage, `2 ≤ k`, and `|V| ≤ 2k+1`. It concludes exactly `q_(k+1) ≤ q_k`.

For an independent marked `k`-set `A` in a finite region `U`, the addable count is `|U|-k-b(A)` where `b(A)` counts its neighbors in `U`. Each marked independent `(k+1)`-set has at least `k` marked-preserving deletions, with one extra precisely when it has at least two marks. The Lean incidence lemma encodes this with the `multiFamily` correction and the strict one-unit loss for each `goodFamily` lower set. The bad/good partition and a fixed non-isolate injection of bad sets into `multiFamily` supply the needed correction at `|U|=2k+1`; in an edgeless `U`, complement supplies the corresponding injection. Thus the proof's stronger relative-region intermediate result has the same intended whole-graph specialization. Empty marked families and ranks beyond graph order remain covered by the same finite-set inequalities.

For the second conjunct, `T.IsTree` and the original degree-one leaf guard imply distinct `v,s`, `H⊆R`, and `|V\H|=|V|-2`. A vertex isolated after deleting `H` has an original neighbor by tree connectivity; that neighbor lies in `H`, cannot be `v`, and is therefore `s`. It is marked by `R\H`. The split lemma gives, at every natural rank `j`, `i_j(T-H)=q_j+i_j(T-R)`. With `k=p-1`, guards `p≥3` and `|T|≤2p+1` become `k≥2` and `|V\H|≤2k+1`.
