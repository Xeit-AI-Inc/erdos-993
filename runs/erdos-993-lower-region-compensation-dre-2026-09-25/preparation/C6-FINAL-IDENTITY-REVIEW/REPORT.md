## Three proposed new identities

### `E993-EQUAL-LENGTH-THREE-SPIDER-EXACT-MODE` — accept as VERIFIED, `proved_informal_no_formal_award`

Exact scope: the ordinary tree with root `r` and `m>=2` internally disjoint length-three paths `r-a_i-b_i-c_i`; strict rise at ranks 0 through `m`, strict fall from rank `m` through terminal nonzero rank `2m`, and first zero-extended strict descent `x=m`. This does not state a mode theorem for unequal or general spiders. Nearest existing identity: `E993-PAIR-SPIDER-CLOSED-FORM`, which supplies a polynomial formula but no mode theorem for this growing equal-leg family; `E993-PAIR-GENERAL-L-DOMINATION` fixes the non-unit background leg multiset while the count of unit legs grows, a different family. Neither subsumes this mode statement. No novelty or priority claim is made.

Here is a self-contained proof check. Set `Q=1+3z+z^2`, `V=1+2z`, and `C=1+z+z^2`. Root exclusion and inclusion give `P_m=Q^m+zV^m`; its degree is `2m` and `alpha=2m`. Every `C^d` is symmetric about rank `d` and weakly rises through rank `d`. This follows by induction from `C^(d+1)=C*C^d`: at `t<d`, the three shifted coefficient differences are nonnegative; at `t=d`, the first two cancel by symmetry and the remaining difference is nonnegative. Expand `Q^m=(C+2z)^m`. At `1<=j<m`, each summand of `Delta_j Q^m` is nonnegative. The summand with `s=j-1` copies of `2z` contributes at least `binom(m,j-1)2^(j-1)`, since `Delta_1 C^(m-j+1)=binom(m-j+1,2)>=1`. This is exactly `[z^j](zV^m)`. Therefore `Delta_j P_m >= [z^(j+1)](zV^m)>0`. Directly, `Delta_0 P_m=3m>0`. Moreover, in `Q^e` for every `e>=1` and `0<=j<e`, the `s=j+1` summand has strictly positive difference, so symmetry gives `Delta_j Q^e<0` for `e<=j<=2e`, including terminal zero extension. At `j=m`, `Delta_m(zV^m)=2^(m-1)(2-m)<=0`; at `j=m+1` it is `-2^m`, and subsequently zero. Hence `Delta_j P_m<0` for `m<=j<=2m`. The proof covers every stated index, including `m=2` and the terminal rank. An independent coefficient calculation for `m=2..60` agreed, but is not a premise.

### `E993-FINITE-GROUP-WEIGHTED-ORBIT-FLOW-LIFT` — accept as VERIFIED, `proved_informal_no_formal_award`, with explicit invariant-weight wording

Exact scope: a finite group acting on finite source and target sets, preserving the bipartite relation **and** the nonnegative integer source supplies and target capacities; the quotient node values are orbit totals and an orbit arc exists exactly when at least one original edge joins the two orbits. The conclusion is conditional on a source-saturating quotient flow. It neither supplies that quotient flow nor proves the OPEN universal weighted Hall claim `E993-LOWER-REGION-TWO-FOR-ONE-WEIGHTED-HALL`. That OPEN claim is the nearest registered flow identity, but it has a different unconditional ordinary-tree assertion. The proposed statement should spell out that `s` and `c` are invariant; its scope already does so. Without invariance the implication is false: two matched source-target pairs swapped by a group, supplies `(2,0)` and capacities `(0,2)`, have feasible orbit totals but no original feasible flow.

For a source orbit `A` and target orbit `B` with a relation edge, group transitivity makes the induced block biregular: every `u in A` has the same block degree `d`, every `v in B` has the same block degree `e`, and `|R_AB|=|A|d=|B|e`. Given quotient flow `f_AB`, assign rational flow `f_AB/|R_AB|` to *each* original edge in that block. Each source in `A` sends `f_AB/|A|` to `B`, and each target in `B` receives `f_AB/|B|` from `A`. Summing block flows and using invariant weights makes every source send exactly its supply and every target receive at most its capacity. Thus a rational feasible saturating flow exists. Give each original relation edge the finite integer capacity equal to total source supply (zero total is immediate). Integral max-flow then gives an integral saturating flow. No free action or edge-transitive block is required. This proof is independent of the reported `T_m` quotient data. The `T_m` orbit formulas and three bounded primal rows are applications only, with no all-parameter quotient-feasibility claim.

The two spider theorems come from the root-origin mathematical proof that was available to the T6 critics; they must not be attributed to the producer's bounded `C6-T6-EQUAL-THREE-SPIDER-REDUCTION` or described as an unreviewed post-critique widening. Its producer result remains formula-plus-532-row bounded evidence. The later T3/F3 envelope is a separate post-critique input. Provenance wording has no effect on the proofs above.
