## 1. Exact terminal intent

Use the first 14 entries of pinned `FirstInteriorMain.lean` (lines 1–247) as the canonical definitions of original leaves, their original support, favorable selection, crossing index, and aggregate. The following is the **proposed exact terminal declaration**, to be placed in a new module after the dependency modules have been made collision-free:

```lean
namespace E993FirstShell

theorem registeredFirstOrderShell
    {V : Type*} [Fintype V] [DecidableEq V]
    (T : SimpleGraph V) [DecidableRel T.Adj]
    (hTree : T.IsTree) (p : ℕ)
    (hEarly : C5LA1.crossingIndex T + 2 ≤ p)
    (hAlpha : 3 * p < 2 * T.indepNum + 1)
    (hOrder : Fintype.card V = 2 * p + 2) :
    C5LA1.aggregate T p ≤ 0

end E993FirstShell
```

This is the declaration **header**, not an elaborated proof. The eventual source must supply a proof and pass a no-`sorry`/no-extra-axiom check. The quantified object is a finite ordinary `SimpleGraph` with `IsTree`; `T.indepNum` is its independence number `α(T)`. `C5LA1.crossingIndex` is `Nat.find` of the first negative `C5LA1.forwardDifferenceDel T ∅`. The strict guard `3*p < 2*α(T)+1` is retained exactly. `Fintype.card V = 2*p+2` is the total **tree** order, not a residual order. `C5LA1.aggregate` (source lines 193–207) sums over the original `leafSet T` filtered by the strict original-vertex-deletion difference `C4LA1.IsFavorableAt T v p`; for each such original leaf it counts `Δ_(p-1)(T-{v,s_v}) - Δ_(p-1)(T-N_T[s_v])`. The `Finset` sum counts distinct leaves even when supports coincide, and an empty favorable set contributes zero. No favored-leaf hypothesis is added to the terminal. Natural subtraction `p - 1` is safe once the proof derives `p ≥ 3`.

The stronger statement that **each** original-leaf term is nonpositive under `hEarly` and `hOrder` is a proposed internal lemma. It has no registered award here. In particular, dropping `hAlpha` from that internal lemma does not alter the terminal statement or the claim key. The only registered target here is the first order shell, not the unrestricted lower-region inequality or larger orders.

## 3. Mathematical lemma DAG, with exact boundary checks

Here `i_j(G)` counts independent `j`-sets and is zero at negative/out-of-range integer ranks; `Δ_j=i_(j+1)-i_j`. For `W⊆V(G)`, `q_j(G,W)` counts independent `j`-sets that meet `W`. `Cover(G,C)` means every graph edge has an endpoint in `C`. Prefer an explicit finite cover predicate and `C.card≤d` in the new code; a separately defined minimum-cover number is unnecessary.

1. **Coefficient and graph bridges.** Prove `C5LA1.indepSetCount T D k` equals `Erdos993G1.indepCount (T.induce {x | x ∉ D}) k` by the subtype/finset bijection, and similarly for the differences. Prove `Fintype.card`/`Nat.card` equality at finite subtype types. For marks, identify `q_j(H,W)=i_j(H)-i_j(H-W)` by a disjoint filter partition at every `j`, including empty and out-of-range strata. These bridges must retain the original parent-graph interpretation of `C5LA1.H`/`R`; they cannot silently replace the terminal aggregate with a new notation.
2. **Unmarked shadow.** For a graph `J` on `m≤2k-1` vertices, `k≥1`, incidence counting gives `k*i_k(J) ≤ (m-k+1)*i_(k-1)(J) ≤ k*i_(k-1)(J)`, interpreted with a natural truncated extension count when `m<k-1`. This yields `i_k≤i_(k-1)` even in empty/out-of-range cases.
3. **Marked A.** For any graph of order `m≤2k`, any `W`, `k≥1`, count deletions of marked independent `(k+1)`-sets: at least `k` deletions preserve a mark; each marked `k`-set has at most `m-k≤k` extensions. Hence `q_(k+1)≤q_k`. Empty `W` is immediate.
4. **Marked B.** If `m≤2k+1`, `k≥2`, and `G` has at least two edges, then `q_(k+1)(G,W)≤q_k(G,W)` for **any** `W`. For `w∈W`, partition `q_j(G,W)=i_(j-1)(G-N[w])+q_j(G-w,W\{w})`. The second term uses A because `|G-w|≤2k`. For the first, `J=G-N[w]` has at most `2k` vertices. At `≤2k-1`, use unmarked shadow. At exactly `2k`, `m=2k+1` and `w` must be isolated; `J=G-w` still has at least two edges. Call first-wide at `r=k-1`, with exact order `2r+2=2k`.
5. **Marked C.** If `|G|=2k+2`, `k≥2`, `G` has no cover of size `≤2` (equivalently `τ(G)≥3`), and `W` contains a vertex `w` with an edge to another vertex of **G**, the same inequality holds. In the partition, `J=G-N[w]` has `≤2k` vertices. At `2k`, `N[w]={w,z}`; every edge removed from `J` touches `z`. If `J` had `≤1` edge, `z` plus one endpoint of that edge would cover `G` with `≤2` vertices. Thus `J` has `≥2` edges and first-wide applies at `r=k-1`. At smaller order use unmarked shadow. The remaining graph `G-w` has order `2k+1` and at least two edges, since otherwise `w` plus one endpoint covers `G`; apply B. This A/B/C route avoids an ordered list of all marks. It is a new formal proof obligation, not an invocation of the older `E993Interior.taggedShadowBound`, whose bipartite high-tail hypotheses differ.
6. **Three-cover rank bound.** Suppose `|T|=2p+2`, `p≥3`, and `C` covers `T` with `|C|≤3`. Extend it to exactly three vertices, possible because `|T|≥8`; set `U=V\C`, `|U|=2p-1`, and `U` is independent. For each independent `X⊆C`, `b_X=|U\N(X)|`. The exact partition is `I_T(z)=Σ_{X⊆C, X independent} z^|X|(1+z)^{b_X}`. At **every** rank `0≤r≤p-2`, use the floor for nonempty `X` and bound the number of size-`a` sets by `choose 3 a` to obtain

   `Δ_r(T) ≥ E(2p-1,r) - 3 c_(r-1) - 3 c_(r-2) - c_(r-3) > 0`,

   where `E(m,j)=choose m (j+1)-choose m j`, `c_j=catalan j` for `j≥0`, and `c_j=0` for `j<0`. In Lean, each subtracted Catalan term needs a guard `if a≤r then catalan (r-a) else 0`; plain natural subtraction would incorrectly turn a negative index into `c_0`. For `r-a=-1`, the **actual** shifted difference is `1` because `choose(m,0)-choose(m,-1)=1`; for `r-a≤-2`, it is `0`. Both are ≥ the stipulated zero lower bound, and neither may be silently treated as a negative Catalan value.

   Since `2p-1≥2r+3`, r25 `binomDelta_mono_center` gives `E(2p-1,r)≥E(2r+3,r)`. At `r=0`, `E(3,0)=2`; at `r=1`, `E(5,1)-3c_0=2`. For `r≥2`, clear denominators in `(r+3)*E(2r+3,r)=2(2r+3)c_(r+1)` to get `E≥2c_(r+1)`. Catalan recurrence gives `c_(j+1)≥2c_j` for `j≥1`, hence `c_(r+1)≥4c_(r-1)`. Also each of the three guarded lower-index terms is at most `c_(r-1)`; their weighted sum is at most `7c_(r-1)`. Thus the margin is at least `c_(r-1)>0`. Formalize the `r=0,1` endpoints separately, then `r≥2` with integer inequalities; do not rely on the registry's informal gamma threshold as a formal lemma.
7. **Early descent excludes the cover.** `C5LA1.crossingIndex T` is the least rank with `Δ<0`, so positivity at every `r≤p-2` implies `p-1≤crossingIndex T`. Combined with `crossingIndex T+2≤p`, this is impossible. Derive `p≥3` from the tree order and `Δ_0(T)=|T|-1>0`: `crossingIndex T≥1`. Spell out the `Nat.find` minimality and positivity bridge; a single positive rank does not suffice.
8. **Leaf cover and marked root.** Let `v` be an original leaf, `s=C5LA1.support T v`, `H=T-{v,s}`, `W=N_T(s)\{v}`, and `R=T-N_T[s]=H-W`. If `H` had a cover of size `≤2`, lift it to the original vertex type and add `s`. This covers all edges of `T`: edges inside `H` are covered by the lifted set; every other edge touches `s` because `v` has only neighbor `s`. It would give a cover of `T` of size `≤3`, contradicting step 7. This direct extension avoids formalizing `α(T)=α(H)+1` or `τ(T)=|T|-α(T)`. Since `H` has no cover of size `≤2`, it has an edge. Connectivity of `T` makes the component of `H` containing that edge attach to `s` through some `w∈W`; the path from `s` to the edge shows `w` has a neighbor **within H**. Acyclicity yields a unique attaching root if useful, but only existence is needed. In particular `W` is nonempty and a mark is nonisolated in `H`; empty-H and all-isolate cases are excluded by the cover bound.
9. **Pointwise then terminal.** `|H|=2p=2(p-1)+2`, `k=p-1≥2`; apply marked C to `(H,W)` and use the coefficient bridge to obtain each original-leaf term `Δ_(p-1)(H)-Δ_(p-1)(R)=q_p(H,W)-q_(p-1)(H,W)≤0`. `Finset.sum_nonpos` over the exact favorable filter proves `C5LA1.aggregate T p≤0`. The alpha guard is a retained terminal hypothesis and unused in this proposed proof. The result remains conditional on completing all bridges and lemmas in Lean; it is **not** a new award.
