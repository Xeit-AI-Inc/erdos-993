## Scope and arithmetic audit

The scope is exactly the first five stored eligible rank-7 rows from the pinned order-16 result and the first five rows at each of orders 17 and 18 in the corrected-scope result. The route does not claim that these are random or representative, nor that it regenerated the census. My replay read copies of the two pinned results and the route code in this scratch area. The output matches the copied sealed evidence byte for byte.

For each row, the route reconstructs a finite ordinary tree from its parenthesized centroid code. Its recurrence computes exact independence-polynomial coefficients. It checks the first strict descent through the zero-extended terminal rank, `alpha`, `x+2<=p`, and `3p<2alpha+1`; here all 15 rows have `alpha=11`, `x=5`, and `p=7`, so `7=5+2` and `21<23`. It recomputes the strict selector on every original leaf, retains distinct leaves that share a support, and uses each original support and closed neighborhood in the full sum. The independent certificate pass also reconstructed the rank-7 and rank-8 layers and verified every positive flow arc, node bound, conservation total, and saturation.

For each selected leaf, the weight is counted on a set only when it contains that leaf and the remaining vertices meet that leaf’s original `W_v`. Thus upper-layer supply is the sum of `q_v(p)` and lower-layer capacity is the sum of `q_v(p-1)`, with `q_v(j)=i_j(T-{v,s_v})-i_j(T-N[s_v])`. Their difference is exactly the contract summand `Delta_(p-1)(T-{v,s_v})-Delta_(p-1)(T-N[s_v])`; the audit checks this identity as a complete sum, not only locally. The two-for-one target removes exactly the two neighbors of the absent vertex from the upper set and inserts that vertex, so it is an independent set of size 7.

All 15 computed primary sums are negative (from -1,262 to -1,811), and all 15 flows saturate upper supply. That is consistent with capacity exceeding supply, and is useful evidence for this routing mechanism on these rows. It does not establish a universal Hall condition; a flow failure elsewhere would also not refute the primary inequality.
