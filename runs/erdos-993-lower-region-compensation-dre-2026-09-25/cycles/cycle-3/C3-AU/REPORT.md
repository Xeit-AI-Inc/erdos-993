# C3-AU neutral adjudication of the U routes

## Mandate and source integrity

This is a proposed worker adjudication of the six C3-U routes and their twelve C3-CT-U/C3-CF-U critics. The two packet inventories were checked byte for byte against their listed SHA-256 values: 66/66 exact case files and 115/115 common files matched. Only packet-listed materials were used. No Lean build, new tree census, large flow replay, external theorem import, or authoritative status change was performed. `RETURN.json` disposes of exactly the twelve source claim IDs. The accompanying `EVIDENCE.json` gives a compact audit and numerical ledger; the mathematical reasons are here.

Write \(i_j(G)\) for zero-extended independent-set counts and \(\Delta_j=i_{j+1}-i_j\). For a finite ordinary tree \(T\), let \(a=\alpha(T)\), \(x=\min\{j\ge0:\Delta_j(T)<0\}\), and take a natural \(p\) with \(x+2\le p\) and strict \(3p<2a+1\). The selector is fixed once in the original tree:

\[
F(T,p)=\{v:\ v\text{ is an original leaf and }\Delta_p(T-v)<0\}.
\]

Each original leaf \(v\) has its original support \(s_v\). Put \(H_v=T-\{v,s_v\}\), \(W_v=N_T(s_v)\setminus\{v\}\), and \(R_v=T-N_T[s_v]=H_v-W_v\). If \(q_v(j)\) counts independent \(j\)-sets of \(H_v\) meeting \(W_v\), then \(q_v(j)=i_j(H_v)-i_j(R_v)\), and the exact target is

\[
S(T,p)=\sum_{v\in F(T,p)}\bigl(q_v(p)-q_v(p-1)\bigr)\le0.
\]

Leaves sharing a support remain distinct summands. For nonempty \(T\), \(\Delta_0=|T|-1\ge0\) and \(\Delta_a=-i_a<0\), so \(x\) is defined with \(x\ge1\), and eligibility gives \(p\ge3\). The strict alpha guard implies \(p\le a-1\); hence \(k=p-1\ge2\), \(h=a-1\), and \(1\le k\le h\). These domain facts govern every reduction below. The empty selector has \(S=0\). The universal lower-region target remains open on these sources.

## U1: clone projection and changed weighted Hall mechanism

**`E993-U1-CLONE-PROJECTION` — retained_narrowed, proposed verified at its exact set-theoretic scope.** For an independent \(r\)-set \(A\subset H_v\) meeting \(W_v\), \(A\cup\{v\}\) is an independent \((r+1)\)-set of \(T\) carrying active tag \(v\). Conversely, an active tag \(v\) on \(B\) forces \(v\in B\), \(s_v\notin B\), and \(A=B-\{v\}\) in that marked residual family. This bijection applies at \(r=p\) and \(r=p-1\). Define \(w_F(B)=|\{v\in F\cap B:(B-\{v\})\cap W_v\ne\varnothing\}|\). Thus the sum of weights on independent \((p+1)\)-sets minus that on independent \(p\)-sets equals \(S(T,p)\), with distinct clone tags even at a common support. The old literal Delete and Retag operations described in the route each project to a one-vertex deletion of underlying sets. The expanded relation joins all clone pairs over a deletion set-pair, and additionally joins pairs over a legal switch. The source's isolated order-14 clone-fiber/switch labels do not contain a complete ordinary-tree edge encoding, so their claimed realization is not accepted. Strict enlargement is retained only for the independently replayed three \(T_m\) instances: the F critic's copied foreground replay found positive flow on 379, 7,231, and 10,987 non-deletion orbit arcs for \((m,p)=(22,34),(60,90),(66,98)\), respectively. That is bounded evidence, not a general strictness theorem for every instance.

**`E993-LOWER-REGION-TWO-FOR-ONE-WEIGHTED-HALL` — retained as an open candidate.** Its upper states are independent \((p+1)\)-sets \(B\), lower states independent \(p\)-sets \(D\), with clone multiplicities \(w_F\). The allowed underlying pairs are every deletion \(D=B-\{b\}\), and every support insertion
\(D=(B\setminus(N_T(s)\cap B))\cup\{s\}\) with \(s\notin B\) and exactly two neighbors of \(s\) in \(B\). The latter removes two vertices and inserts one, so it is independent and has size \(p\). All clone pairs over an allowed underlying pair are joined. A matching saturating all upper clones implies \(S\le0\) by the preceding count identity. It is stronger than the scalar target because it requires every subset cut, not merely total upper mass at most total lower mass.

The copied common instrument reports full expanded quotient flow and zero cut deficit for the three \(T_m\) rows: upper supply/flow \(6,533,318,342,644,086,823,410\), \(18,218,546,853,926,351,167,830,731,257,168,063,662,695,945,613,618,170,468,520\), and \(10,621,175,131,261,862,108,643,497,325,635,134,690,753,959,446,180,514,839,346,249,972\). The F critic replayed the copied instrument and its small \(m=1,2,3\) literal-neighbor smoke checks; the T critic did not replay it and correctly noted that the case files did not contain the per-instance primal/cut artifacts. These views are compatible: the replay supports those three finite outcomes, while the missing per-instance files prevent a separate artifact-level audit here. The reported 241 eligible \(p=6\) flows are a separate bounded result and were not rerun in this seat. The old T22 deletion-only cut deficit \(14,226,520,737,620,288,370\) concerns the old relation; that same row has full \(S=-498,754,180,547,001,418,536\), and its expanded flow saturates. The supplied CB(8,92) envelope is inconclusive. No universal Hall proof, new-graph countercut, or positive complete \(S\) follows. An old-relation refutation or governed `RTree` result has no transfer bridge to this changed ordinary-tree relation.

## U2: rank-at-most-seven composition

**`E993-LOWER-REGION-AGGREGATE-RANK-LE-SEVEN` — retained_narrowed to an explicit census-dependent composition, proposed open.** Its exact conclusion is \(S(T,p)\le0\) for every eligible ordinary tree at \(p\le7\). The supporting chain has an informal order-band lemma and bounded-computation inputs, so the finite data are not silently promoted to a formal theorem.

First, for a graph \(G\) of order \(m\le2k+1\), marks \(W\) containing every isolate, and \(k\ge2\), the marked counts obey \(q_{k+1}\le q_k\). Indeed, for a marked independent \(k\)-set \(A\), let \(b(A)=|N_G(A)|\), and let \(C\) count independent \((k+1)\)-sets with at least two marks once each. Counting actual addable extensions gives
\(kq_{k+1}+C=(m-k)q_k-\sum_A b(A)\), hence \(k(q_{k+1}-q_k)=(m-2k)q_k-\sum_A b(A)-C\). The result is immediate for \(m\le2k\). At \(m=2k+1\), good marked \(k\)-sets have \(b(A)\ge1\); bad ones consist wholly of isolates. If a nonisolated vertex \(y\) exists, each bad \(A\) injects into a multiply marked independent \(A\cup\{y\}\) counted by \(C\), because \(k\ge2\) and all isolates are marked. If none exists, \(G\) is edgeless and \(W=V(G)\), so complementing subsets gives equality. For a leaf residual \(H_v\), each isolate lies in \(W_v\) by connectivity of the original tree. At \(|T|\le2p+1\), \(|H_v|\le2(p-1)+1\); thus *every* original-leaf summand is nonpositive, independent of favorability.

Second, the registered forest first-descent maxima \(M(1),\ldots,M(5)=(4,8,11,15,18)\), supported by bounded census together with the separate global \(4k\) descent-order bound, imply \(|T|\le M(x)\). Since \(1\le x\le p-2\) and these five entries increase, the ceilings for \(p=3,4,5,6,7\) are \(4,8,11,15,18\). The order band covers \(p=3,4,5\) wholly, and leaves precisely \((p,n)=(6,14),(6,15),(7,16),(7,17),(7,18)\). The pinned finite protocols use complete free-tree generation by centroid codes, exact original-tree selector and zero-extended first-descent filters, coefficient checks, and independent vertex-subset replay for every eligible row. ## U3: marked-cover and first-shell proofs

**`C3-U3-MARKED-COVER-SHELL` — retained, proposed informal.** Exact statement: if \(G\) is a finite simple graph with \(|G|=2k+2\), \(k\ge2\), vertex-cover number \(\tau(G)\ge3\), and \(W\) contains a nonisolated vertex, then \(q_{k+1}(G,W)\le q_k(G,W)\). Choose such a \(w\in W\). The disjoint split \(q_j(G,W)=i_{j-1}(G-N[w])+q_j(G-w,W-\{w\})\) uses no earlier neighborhoods. A graph of order at most \(2k\) has \(q_{k+1}\le q_k\) by marked-preserving deletion/extension incidence: each upper marked set has at least \(k\) qualifying deletions and a lower set at most \(|G|-k\le k\) extensions. For the auxiliary order-at-most-\(2k+1\) case with at least two edges, split at any mark. Its second term has order at most \(2k\). Its first residual has order at most \(2k\); if at most \(2k-1\), ordinary incidence gives \(i_k\le i_{k-1}\). At exactly \(2k\), the mark is isolated and the residual keeps at least two edges, so the registered exact first-wide graph classification with parameter \(r=k-1\ge1\) gives \(i_k\le i_{k-1}\).

For the target order \(2k+2\), the nonisolated \(w\) gives \(|G-N[w]|\le2k\). If equality holds, exactly \(w\) and its sole neighbor \(z\) were deleted. A residual with at most one edge would make \(\{z\}\) plus an endpoint of that edge a cover of size at most two, contradicting \(\tau(G)\ge3\); hence the exact first-wide result applies. Smaller residuals use ordinary incidence. The graph \(G-w\) has order \(2k+1\) and at least two edges by the same cover argument, so the auxiliary bound applies to the second split term. This proves the statement. The first-wide dependency is only the registered exact-order assertion: on \(2r+2\) vertices, \(r\ge1\), \(\Delta_r>0\) iff the graph has at most one edge. The proof has no larger-order implication and no formal award here.

**`C3-U3-FIRST-SHELL-POINTWISE` — retained, proposed informal.** Exact statement: for an ordinary tree with \(p\ge3\), \(|T|=2p+2\), and \(x+2\le p\), every original-leaf summand \(q_v(p)-q_v(p-1)\le0\), hence so is the sum over the unchanged selector. Suppose \(\tau(T)\le3\), and extend a cover to a 3-set \(C\). Its complement \(U\) is independent of size \(2p-1\). Splitting each independent set by its part \(X\subseteq C\) yields \(I(T,z)=\sum_{X\subseteq C,\ X\text{ independent}}z^{|X|}(1+z)^{b_X}\). With \(E(N,r)=\binom N{r+1}-\binom Nr\) and Catalan \(\mathrm{Cat}_j=0\) at negative indices, the registered binomial-difference Catalan floor bounds every nonempty \(X\) contribution to \(\Delta_r\) below by \(-\mathrm{Cat}_{r-|X|}\). For all \(0\le r\le p-2\), monotonicity of \(E(N,r)\) at \(N\ge2r+3\) gives
\[
\Delta_r(T)\ge E(2r+3,r)-3\mathrm{Cat}_{r-1}-3\mathrm{Cat}_{r-2}-\mathrm{Cat}_{r-3}>0.
\]
The margins at \(r=0,1\) are 2. At \(r\ge2\), \(E(2r+3,r)=2(2r+3)\mathrm{Cat}_{r+1}/(r+3)\ge2\mathrm{Cat}_{r+1}\ge8\mathrm{Cat}_{r-1}\), while the subtractions total at most \(7\mathrm{Cat}_{r-1}\). Positivity at *every* earlier rank contradicts \(x\le p-2\); hence \(\tau(T)\ge4\).

For each original leaf, pendant exchange gives \(\alpha(H_v)=a-1\), so \(\tau(H_v)=\tau(T)-1\ge3\). Each component of \(H_v\) contains the vertex formerly adjacent to \(s_v\) in \(W_v\); since \(H_v\) has an edge, some such marked vertex is nonisolated. Now \(|H_v|=2p=2(p-1)+2\), so the preceding marked-cover theorem at \(k=p-1\) yields \(q_v(p)\le q_v(p-1)\). This needs neither the alpha-region guard nor favorability to establish the pointwise sign, but the registered aggregate consequence retains the strict guard and fixed selector. Both critics checked the cover, residual edge, and rank boundaries. The route report's sentence saying it had no claim IDs conflicts with its RETURN, which lists both; it is clerical and does not alter these dispositions. This shell argument does not extend to \(|T|\ge2p+3\).

## U4: exact transport reductions and literature scope

**`C3-U4.CUT-DUAL` — retained, proposed verified as a finite-network fact.** For finite upper states with supplies \(u(B)\ge0\), lower states with capacities \(c(D)\ge0\), and uncapacitated allowed arcs, full supply saturation is equivalent by max-flow/min-cut to \(u(X)\le c(N(X))\) for every upper-state subset \(X\). Integer capacities yield integral flow. The deficit \(g(X)=c(N(X))-u(X)\) is submodular because nonnegative weighted neighborhood coverage is submodular and \(u\) is modular. Global minimizers of \(g\) are closed under union and intersection. None of this establishes that all tree-derived cuts pass. Even a failed cut would leave the scalar target undecided.

**`C3-U4.ORBIT-COMPRESSION` — retained, proposed verified at fixed-instance scope.** A finite group preserving supplies, capacities and legal arcs gives a quotient on upper/lower vertex orbits. Each nonempty orbit-pair relation is biregular. An orbit flow \(f_{ij}\) can be spread evenly over all literal edges of that block, making every upper vertex send \(f_{ij}/|U_i|\) and every lower vertex receive \(f_{ij}/|L_j|\). Thus quotient feasibility lifts to literal fractional feasibility; finite integer max flow makes it integral where supplies/capacities are integers. Conversely, aggregate a literal flow by orbit. Tree automorphisms preserve original leaves, supports, \(\Delta_p(T-v)\), the fixed selector, weights, and legal deletion/switch moves. The quotient therefore preserves saturation feasibility for each fixed tree instance, but need not be small and gives no universal cut inequality.

**`C3-U4.COLORED-SHADOW-SCOPE` — retained_narrowed to a direct-application check, proposed verified.** A one-step subset shadow of a family consists of contained lower-rank subsets. A legal switch here inserts a vertex absent from the upper state, and both sides carry selector-dependent capacities. Thus an unweighted subset-shadow cardinality statement, including the colored-shadow theorem described by the route, does not *by itself* prove the present weighted deletion-plus-switch cut inequalities. This says nothing about a future bridging argument or other uses of the cited paper. Neither critic independently retrieved its precise primary-paper theorem; no positive mathematical conclusion here depends on that citation.

## U5: early-descent transfer condition and exact target

**`C3-U5-EARLY-DESCENT-MARKED-OCCUPANCY-BRIDGE` — retained, proposed verified as a conditional implication.** Let \(k=p-1\). For an independent set \(A\), let \(e_G(A)\) count *actual* addable vertices. Double counting extensions at the first strict descent gives \(E_x=(x+1)i_{x+1}(T)/i_x(T)<x+1\le k\), where \(i_x>0\). Let \(Q=\sum_{v\in F}q_v(k)\), and \(E\) be the total \(e_{H_v}(A)\) over all selected-leaf-tagged marked independent \(k\)-sets \(A\). The additional premise is precisely \(E\le E_x Q\); it is not established universally.

For each \(H_v\), bipartiteness and \(h=\alpha(H_v)=a-1\) bound actual addability by \(2(h-k)\): within either color class, all addable vertices together with \(A\) are independent. Define \(D_v=2(h-k)q_v(k)-\sum_A e_{H_v}(A)\ge0\). Let \(C_v\ge0\) count independent \((k+1)\)-sets meeting \(W_v\) in at least two vertices, once per set. Singly marked upper sets have exactly \(k\) deletions leaving a marked lower set, and multiply marked ones have \(k+1\). Hence \(kq_v(k+1)+C_v=2(h-k)q_v(k)-D_v\). Summing gives the exact identity
\[
kS(T,p)=(2a+1-3p)Q-\sum_{v\in F}(D_v+C_v).
\]
Under the added transfer premise and \(Q>0\), \(E<kQ\), so \(\sum D_v=2(h-k)Q-E>(2(h-k)-k)Q=(2a+1-3p)Q\) and \(S<0\). At \(Q=0\), the identity directly gives \(S\le0\). The rank domain is valid by the opening guards. This proof is a conditional reduction, not a universal budget.

**`E993-LOWER-REGION-ORDINARY-FAVORABLE-LEAF-AGGREGATE` — retained_narrowed as the exact registered open target.** U5 restates the full \(S(T,p)\le0\) assertion for every eligible ordinary tree, with the strict original selector and distinct same-support tags. Its only path to a full proof is the preceding condition, for which no universal comparison between marked rank-\(p-1\) sets in the deletion graphs and unmarked rank-\(x\) sets in \(T\) is given. No positive complete-sum counterexample is supplied. Nonnegativity of \(D_v,C_v\) alone is insufficient when \(2a+1-3p>0\). The U2 finite rank conclusion, U3 first shell, and U6 stars do not cover all \(p\) and orders.

## U6: marked down operator and stars

**`C3-U6.marked-down-operator` — retained as a conditional criterion, proposed open for the missing injectivity premise.** Let \(M_j(H,W)\) be the rational vector space with basis the independent \(j\)-sets meeting \(W\); its dimension is \(q_j(H,W)\). The map \(d_p:M_p\to M_{p-1}\) sends a basis set to the sum of its one-vertex deletions that still meet \(W\). It is well-defined. If this map is injective for every selected \((H_v,W_v)\), dimensions give \(q_v(p)\le q_v(p-1)\), hence each selected summand and their complete sum are nonpositive. General eligible-tree injectivity has not been proved. At the unselected \(K_{1,20}\) residual rank \(d_6\), dimensions \(\binom{19}{6}=27,132>\binom{19}{5}=11,628\) obstruct injectivity; this does not test an eligible rank or refute the primary.

**`C3-U6.star-subcase` — retained, proposed verified for eligible ordinary stars.** For \(T=K_{1,m}\), \(m\ge4\), \(a=m\), \(i_0=1\), \(i_1=m+1\), \(i_j=\binom mj\) for \(2\le j\le m\), and \(i_j=0\) above \(m\). Here \(\Delta_0=m\), \(\Delta_1=\binom m2-(m+1)\ge0\), \(\Delta_j=\binom mj(m-2j-1)/(j+1)\) for \(2\le j<m\), and terminal \(\Delta_m=-1\). Thus \(x=\lfloor(m-1)/2\rfloor+1\). For every \(p\) satisfying both original guards, deletion of any leaf gives \(K_{1,m-1}\) with \(\Delta_p<0\), so all \(m\) leaves are in \(F\). For each leaf \(H_v\) is edgeless on \(m-1\) vertices, \(W_v=V(H_v)\), and \(R_v\) is empty. Because \(p-1\ge x+1>(m-1)/2\) and the alpha guard keeps \(p\le m-1\), each term is \(\binom{m-1}{p}-\binom{m-1}{p-1}<0\). Therefore
\(S=m[\binom{m-1}{p}-\binom{m-1}{p-1}]<0\), counting all shared-support leaves separately. The exact supplied examples \((m,p)=(20,12),(20,13)\) give \(-503,880\) and \(-465,120\). For small \(m\) without an eligible \(p\), the quantified subcase is vacuous. No arbitrary-tree conclusion follows.

## Adjudicated boundary

Every source claim has a disposition above and in `RETURN.json`. The critics' differences concern evidence grade and example realization, not a competing proof or positive full-sum counterexample. The surviving exact contributions are the clone identity and bounded changed-graph successes; the census-dependent rank-through-seven composition; the informal first-shell pointwise proof; general cut/orbit facts; a conditional marked-occupancy budget; a conditional operator criterion; and the exact star family. The unrestricted strict lower-region selected aggregate remains open in this portfolio. A future proof needs a uniform global deficit budget or another complete-sum argument at orders/ranks beyond those covered; a refutation needs one fully encoded eligible ordinary tree, complete strict selector, zero-extended \(x\), all leaf summands, and positive full \(S\). No route supplied either.
