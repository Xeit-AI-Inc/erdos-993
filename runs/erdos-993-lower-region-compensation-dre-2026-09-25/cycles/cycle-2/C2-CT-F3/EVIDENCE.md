The finite family is generated by rooted unlabeled branch-tree types represented recursively as sorted child-type tuples. A support 0 and distinguished leaf 1 are fixed; a multiset of rooted branches of total order 2p is attached to 0. The filter retains branch multisets with at least two components and at least two distinct rooted types. The result is a rooted-family count, not a count of unrooted free trees; isomorphic unrooted trees may recur.

## Audit of formulas and guards

The copied evaluator zero-extends coefficients: `coefficient` returns zero outside the polynomial's degree, and `delta` is `i_(j+1)-i_j`. Its first-descent loop includes rank `len(poly)-1`, so it checks the terminal difference `0-i_alpha<0`; this is the required terminal descent. For each row the script first computes the tree polynomial and alpha, then checks `x+2<=p` and `3p<2alpha+1`. It forms the favorable set over every original leaf using strict `Delta_p(T-v)<0`. `leaf_data` uses the original support and original closed neighborhood to build H and R, and computes `Delta_(p-1)(H)-Delta_(p-1)(R)`. Summands are retained per leaf, so same-support leaves have their full multiplicity. The aggregate is the sum of precisely those favorable-leaf entries.

The generator's sorted-multiset recursion enumerates the specified rooted type multisets with replacement and no ordering duplicates. The `len(f)<2` and `len(set(f))<2` filters implement the stated branch-count and distinct-type restrictions. All these checks were also exercised by the successful replay; this critique did not independently enumerate trees outside that generator.

## Disposition boundary

The exact replay supports the stated finite claim for this generated family and orders. It does not cover symmetric branch multisets, single-component or identical-type products, p outside 3 through 7, or arbitrary ordinary trees at n=2p+2. It proves neither the full registered shell assertion nor its negation, and provides no positive local term or complete-sum counterexample.
