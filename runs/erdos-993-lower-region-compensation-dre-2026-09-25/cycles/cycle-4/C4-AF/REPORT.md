# C4-AF neutral adjudication of the F portfolio

This is a proposed, nonauthoritative adjudication of the six C4-F routes and their twelve listed T/U critics. I checked all 179 common and 112 case-file SHA-256 pins against the packet inventories; none was absent or different. I used the five common read-first controls and only packet-listed current sources. The exact source-claim union has seven IDs, each disposed of below. Source prose mistakes are kept separate from the exact surviving RETURN statements. No route supplies a proof or refutation of the ordinary-tree lower-region aggregate. Finite checks have their stated populations only.

## Target and arithmetic boundary

For a finite ordinary tree T, write a=alpha(T), Delta_j(T)=i_(j+1)(T)-i_j(T) with zero extension, and x for the first nonnegative j with Delta_j(T)<0. The registered target uses p>=x+2 and strict 3p<2a+1. Its selector is the **fixed original-leaf set** F={v:Delta_p(T-v)<0}. Each selected original leaf has its own original support s_v and summand Delta_(p-1)(T-{v,s_v})-Delta_(p-1)(T-N_T[s_v]); equal supports do not merge leaf tags. The sought full selected sum S is <=0. All finite eligible rows below were checked against this ordinary-tree convention, and none has positive S.

For each selected tag put H_v=T-{v,s_v}, W_v=N_T(s_v)\{v}, R_v=H_v-W_v, k=p-1, q_v(j)=i_j(H_v)-i_j(R_v), Q=sum q_v(k), U=sum q_v(k+1). Let E be the **actual** number of addable vertices summed over marked independent k-sets in each H_v, and C the number of marked independent (k+1)-sets meeting W_v at least twice, counted once per upper set and tag. Double counting marked addability gives E=kU+C: an upper set with one mark has k tag-preserving deletions, while one with multiple marks has k+1. Hence kS=E-C-kQ. These identities must use H_v, not the undeleted T. CT_x is the distinct inequality i_x(T)E<=(x+1)i_(x+1)(T)Q. The flat gap is (x+1)Q-E; the current-rank gap is kQ-E. At p=x+2 the flat and current gaps coincide, but CT_x does not, since i_(x+1)<i_x. No route proves the global compensation budget.

## Unresolved obligations and recommendation

All seven source claims have dispositions above. The F1, F2, F4, F5 bounded, and F6 calculations are finite; F3 is one exact tree; the all-m T_m statement is open. The primary needs a proof of the complete fixed-selector sum for every eligible ordinary tree, or an eligible tree with exact positive complete S and all original-leaf terms. None is present. In the marked-incidence route, a proof of E-C<=kQ or an adequate global D+C budget must handle actual H_v addability and the full selected tag set; CT_x, flat and current-rank bounds are distinct. A local positive term, a failed sufficient mechanism, a negative finite sample, or a family coefficient reduction does not decide the primary. The high-tail and governed-tree scopes remain separate. I recommend the controller retain the exact bounded/one-instance observations and leave the primary and all-m family OPEN.
