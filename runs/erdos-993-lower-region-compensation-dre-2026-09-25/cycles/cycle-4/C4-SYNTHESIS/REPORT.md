## Exact algebra and a genuine subcase

Let `Q=sum_F q_v(k)`, `U=sum_F q_v(k+1)`, `E` count actual addable vertices over the marked independent `k`-sets in the tagged `H_v`, and `C` count each tagged upper set with at least two marks once. Double counting yields `E=kU+C`, hence `kS=E-C-kQ`. For an open-neighborhood sum `M=sum_(v,A)|N_(H_v)(A)|` over those marked sets, addability also gives `E=(n-2-k)Q-M`, where `n=|T|`. Therefore

`kS=(n-2p)Q-M-C`.

This is the C4-T2 exact reduction, consistent with C4-U1's tagged occupancy and C4-T4's relative-face dimension identity. It proves the eligible subcase `n<=2p`, since `Q,M,C>=0` and `k>=1`. For `n>2p`, the precise missing global budget is `M+C>=(n-2p)Q`, equivalently `E-C<=kQ`. The stronger current-rank budget `E<=kQ`, flat budget `E<=(x+1)Q`, and CT_x premise `i_x E<=(x+1)i_(x+1)Q` have not been established universally. C4-T1 proves only that CT_x, if true on a given eligible pair, implies its `S<=0`: for `Q>0`, first descent gives `i_x>i_(x+1)` and `k>=x+1`; for `Q=0`, `U=0`. C4-U1 rewrites CT_x as a covariance budget when `Q>0`; its own-support contribution does not control the other terms. These are separate mechanisms, not substitutes for the complete sum.
