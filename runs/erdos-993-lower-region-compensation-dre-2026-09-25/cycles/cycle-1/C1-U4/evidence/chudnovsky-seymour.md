# Primary source checked for the spectral route

Maria Chudnovsky and Paul Seymour, “The Roots of the Independence Polynomial of a Clawfree Graph,” *Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B* 97 (2007), 350–357. Primary manuscript: https://web.math.princeton.edu/~pds/papers/roots/roots.pdf

The paper states Theorem 1.1: if `G` is claw-free, all roots of `I(G,x)` are real. Its introduction defines claw-free as having no induced claw and gives `K_{1,3}` with `I(K_{1,3},x)=1+4x+3x^2+x^3` as a graph whose independence polynomial does not have all roots real. Application check: arbitrary trees in the assigned target can contain induced `K_{1,3}`; the theorem's hypothesis is not part of the target. The theorem also concerns a single graph's polynomial, not the selected sum of mixed finite differences derived in `REPORT.md`.
