# Third-shell low-cover redirection (controller reasoning, unreviewed)

For n=2p+4 and p10..13, do not try to extend the false universal tau(T)>=7 threshold. The encoded n24,p10,x8,alpha18,cover6 tree already disproves it while its full aggregate remains negative. The small-rank degree-budget calculation gives positive cover exclusion at p6..9, but four boundary cells at p10..13 fail. A useful successor separates high-cover trees (the existing marked matching5 reduction) from low-cover exceptions.

A six-vertex cover permits an exact polynomial decomposition with at most64 cover states and an independent outside set of size n-6. In a tree, outside vertices of degree>=2 connect cover vertices without cycles; there are at most5 such connector vertices, since sum(deg(u)-1) over all outside vertices is at most5. The remaining outside vertices are pendant leaves or, in a connected tree with edges, never isolated. Thus after choosing the finite cover/connector skeleton, unbounded data are only six leaf multiplicities. At fixed n24..30 the multiplicities are finite compositions. This suggests an exact compressed census of low-cover exceptions with a proof of skeleton completeness, or symbolic coefficient inequalities per skeleton. It is not yet a completed instrument or theorem. Cover vertices may themselves be original leaves; reconstruct the FULL original degree-one set from edges to avoid repeating the C3-T6 omission.

The matching threshold cannot simply be weakened: on 2k+2 vertices, four disjoint edges plus isolates may have i_k>i_(k-1). At k4, 4K2+2K1 has i4=104>i3=88. Extra graph edges cannot be invoked through refuted edge-addition monotonicity. Any improved argument must use actual low-cover tree structure, the early-descent condition, or a subset-preserving injection proved for that structure.

A complete six-cover enumeration must account for edges inside the cover, outside connector neighborhoods, tree connectivity/acyclicity, canonicalization or explicit overcounting, and exact original selector. Overcounting is harmless for a universal finite check if coverage is proved; omissions are not. First establish a manageable skeleton count and completeness argument before committing to runtime.
