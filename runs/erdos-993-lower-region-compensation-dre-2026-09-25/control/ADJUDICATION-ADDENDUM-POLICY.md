The controller found a concrete set-deletion error in the completed C2-AU adjudication. A fresh GPT-6 Sol high repair seat will re-audit that U-portfolio disposition against its original allowed source case and the controller's explicit scope counterexample. This is a correction of one logical origin adjudication, not a fourth voting portfolio or a new search candidate. Stage6 is held until the repair has finished and its exact outputs have passed controller admission.

Preserve every original adjudicator and critic byte and seal. The repair's self-contained report, result and any evidence will be added under fresh top-level filenames in the C2-AU portfolio, with explicit producer identity and a separate addendum manifest. Existing files are not edited. The Stage5 seal will bind both the original admission and the addendum. The synthesis case will include the three original adjudicator portfolios and the U portfolio's pinned correction, must reconcile their statements explicitly, and may not read raw routes or critics. Its claim-coverage set remains the union of the three adjudicator RETURNs. The repair introduces no new target and cannot award the primary. All newly copied files are admitted only after their producer is finished and a process/source-integrity check passes.

This documented amendment allows versioned top-level corrective evidence within a sealed portfolio without pretending the original adjudication was correct or overwriting any sealed member. It does not weaken source pinning, exact coverage, model policy, review independence, or the 18/36/3/1 logical topology. Extra repair transport is counted separately in the final execution record.
