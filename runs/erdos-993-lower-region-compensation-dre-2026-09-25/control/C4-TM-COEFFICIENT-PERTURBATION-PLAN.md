# T_m coefficient perturbation plan (unproved controller direction)

Let B=1+4z+3z^2+z^3, L=1+z. The exact parent polynomial is P=A+J with A=(1+2z)B^m and J=zL^(3m+1). At z1, A(1)=3*9^m and J(1)=2*8^m, so the total-mass ratio J(1)/A(1)=(2/3)(8/9)^m decays exponentially. This total-mass comparison alone does NOT bound coefficient differences in the tail or near a flat mode.

When both leaf orbits are favorable, Q=(3m+1)zL^(3m)+3m z(2+z)(1+2z)B^(m-1). The second term's total mass is3m*9^m; the first is(3m+1)*8^m. Their ratio also decays exponentially. A potential all-m proof is a rigorously quantified log-concave-main-term plus perturbation argument, with an explicit finite threshold and exact finite verification below it. The difficult part is a uniform lower bound on the NEGATIVE adjacent main-term difference at every required rank, connected to the actual first strict descent of P, and a bound on the positive correction difference. A sum of log-concave terms is not automatically log-concave or unimodal.

Mean computations are exact but not mode bounds: A has mean13m/9+2/3; J has mean(3m+3)/2; the main Q term has mean13m/9+5/9. The parent/correction means separate by orderm/18, so an adjacent-mode theorem cannot simply be applied to the two summands for allm. B is log-concave but not real-rooted (discriminant-31), and its normalized binomial coefficients [1,4/3,1,1] are not ultra-log-concave (the middle inequality at index2 fails). Do not use Darroch or a Poisson-binomial representation for B.

Possible rigorous tools: convolution preservation for actual log-concave factors; exact recurrence for B^m coefficients; certified coefficient-ratio inequalities; a uniform local limit or saddle-point estimate with explicit error covering the needed region, plus separate tails and smallm. Any asymptotic estimate must be uniform in p, not only at a fixed proportion, and must prove both original orbit selectors before using Qall. A fresh asymmetric-claw variant is a useful falsification of any proposed mechanism. No theorem or status is asserted by this plan.
