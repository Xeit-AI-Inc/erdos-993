# Controller formalization hint, not a C2 search input

The ordered-first-mark proof can be replaced by a two-part decomposition once a marked non-isolated vertex y is available. Partition by whether y belongs to the independent set:
q_j(G,W)=i_(j-1)(G-N[y])+q_j(G-y,W\{y}).
At m<=2k+1, the first graph has at most2k-1 vertices, so its unmarked k-layer is no larger than its (k-1)-layer. The second graph has at most2k vertices, so the elementary marked shadow inequality k*q_(k+1)<=(m_prime-k)*q_k implies its marked (k+1)-layer is no larger than its k-layer. Thus no ordered list of all marks is required. Prove both finite-set splits with exact original-deletion conventions.

This is only a simplification strategy for the registered ordinary-leaf order band, not a new award or a change to its p>=3 contract. It does not establish the generic all-isolates-marked theorem when no marked vertex is non-isolated. The ordinary-tree structural disjunction (some nonisolated marked root, or H edgeless and allmarked) is still needed. The arbitrary marked even-order shadow and ordinary unmarked shadow should handle out-of-range ranks explicitly rather than use Nat subtraction to conceal a deficit.
