# Controller-admitted DRE worker and handoff protocol

Both identities are registered OPEN before route work. The controller approves final packets and hashes and owns all verdicts. At most six cycles are authorized. A cycle has 18 isolated routes (`T1..T6`, `F1..F6`, `U1..U6`), two cross-orientation critics for each route (36), three origin adjudicators, and one neutral synthesis. Routes and critics use GPT-6 Luna high; adjudication and synthesis use GPT-6 Sol high. Controller Astra high reviews after cycles 3 and 6. Each seat gets a fresh isolated context, no delegated agents, and no sibling or unrelated live output.

## Inputs and allowed work

Every route receives the same `SOLUTION-CONTRACT.md` and `NEUTRAL-HANDOFF.md`, authorized source pins, and a role/lens wrapper. Broad, genuinely different mathematical approaches are allowed; role labels do not narrow the target. Public primary mathematical literature may be retrieved and cited with exact propositions, hypotheses, and application checks. No unverified imported theorem, search failure, vote, local sign, or bounded census becomes a status. Routes may prove, refute, or return explicit partial results. No route self-awards a claim.

Each critic receives the common handoff and exactly one sealed route case. The critic checks the full target statement, rank and graph domains, all selector values, arithmetic, proof gaps, citation bridges, and the imported counterexample fences. For each route claim, the critic records one disposition in its `reason`: `retained`, `retained_narrowed`, or `rejected`. `retained_narrowed` carries the revised exact statement and scope; `rejected` records the decisive defect without pretending the underlying mathematical assertion is refuted. Its `status` always begins `proposed_`.

An origin adjudicator receives exactly its six origin routes and their twelve cross-orientation critics, with source files pinned. It disposes of every source claim using the same three dispositions in `reason` and proposed status, reconciles critic disagreements from evidence, and carries forward the exact surviving statements, scopes, limitations, source dependence, and proof gaps. It neither reads other orientations' adjudications nor awards an authoritative verdict. Synthesis receives **only the three sealed origin adjudications** as worker sources, plus the common control/capsule inputs. Since it cannot reopen routes or critics, adjudicators must make their carried scopes and evidence self-contained. Synthesis covers every adjudicator claim, states any unresolved gaps and conflicting scopes, and proposes an evidence-based recommendation for controller review. No vote across seats resolves the primary.

## File layout and exact return contract

Workers write proposed files only beneath their assigned absolute scratch directory. The controller copies admitted files without revising mathematical claims into the final `cycles/cycle-N/ID/` directory, records a receipt and pins file hashes. Every worker's final return is `cycles/cycle-N/ID/RETURN.json`; all paths inside that return are root-relative **final admission paths**, even while its bytes are first written in scratch. Write REPORT.md and RETURN.json directly at the assigned scratch root (not nested final directories); reference their final admission paths in JSON. A report is required, and all evidence files must be inside the same final worker directory. No background writer survives return. The controller validates every return and dependency case with the proposed `worker_ops.py` checks before sealing.

The JSON object must have exactly `worker_id`, `cycle`, `stage`, `orientation`, `claims`, `report_path`, `limitations`, and `status_authority`; non-search stages additionally require `covered_claim_ids`. `worker_id` patterns are `C{N}-{T|F|U}{1..6}` for routes, `C{N}-C{critic}-{origin}{1..6}` for critics with different critic and origin, `C{N}-A{origin}` for adjudicators, and `C{N}-SYNTHESIS`, where `1<=N<=6`. Stages are respectively `search`, `critique`, `adjudication`, `synthesis`; orientations are respectively route orientation, critic orientation, `N`, `N`. `cycle` is an integer equal to `N`. `status_authority` is exactly `proposed_worker_only`. `claims` and `limitations` are arrays; every limitation is nonempty text.

Each `claims` element has **exactly** `claim_id`, `statement`, `scope`, `status`, `evidence_paths`, `reason`. Claim IDs are unique and match `[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._:-]*`; statement, scope and reason are nonempty. `status` is a nonempty `proposed_*` string, such as `proposed_open`, `proposed_verified`, or `proposed_refuted` according to the worker's evidence grade; it is never authoritative. Evidence paths are distinct root-relative files inside the final worker directory. The report path is also such a file. Search returns omit `covered_claim_ids` entirely. Every review return's `covered_claim_ids` is a unique array that matches **exactly** the union of source `claims[].claim_id` values determined by its sealed dependency inventory, with no extra or missing IDs; each covered ID also appears in the reviewer's own `claims` as a disposition. Reviewers may add clearly identified derived claims, but may not use them to evade source coverage.

Examples of complete source sets: `C1-CF-T1` gets route `C1-T1`; `C1-AT` gets `C1-T1..T6` and critics `C1-CF-T1..T6`, `C1-CU-T1..T6`; `C1-SYNTHESIS` gets only `C1-AT`, `C1-AF`, `C1-AU`. The controller's case generator computes the exact covered IDs from pinned returns, rather than assuming one claim per source.

`worker_ops.py` rejects duplicate JSON keys, nonfinite numbers, forbidden authority/time/hash keys anywhere (`timestamp`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, `wallclock`, `wall_clock`, `claim_hash`, `claim_sha256`, `registry_status`, `authoritative_status`), identity mismatches, invalid paths and missing files. Inventory source files must be hash-pinned, live inside their final worker directory, and include each referenced report/evidence file. Synthesis source inventories accept only top-level adjudicator files. Return data carries no elapsed time. Preserve any original source return on admission and normalize representation only with an explicit receipt, never by rewriting a worker's mathematical claim.

## Report requirements and status boundary

Reports use plain proof sketches and checkable evidence, not private deliberations. Separate established steps from conjectures and missing bridges. Verify `x(T)` through the terminal zero-extended rank, `x+2<=p`, `3p<2a+1`, each strict `Delta_p(T-v)<0`, `p-1`, original support and neighborhood, all same-support leaf tags, and the complete global sum. Algebra using `D,C` must count actual addable vertices and multiply marked sets once and must prove the global budget if claiming the primary. A positive term/fibre does not refute a global claim; an ineligible row does not decide it. Computational reports give exact integers, seed and graph recipe, finite generation boundary and count, replay instructions, and the limited conclusion. Imported results require primary citation and a checked hypothesis bridge. Gaps remain explicit in `limitations` and reports. Root alone decides what to register, promote, or leave OPEN after reviewing sealed evidence.

Controller additions: use inputs/ordinary_tree_checked.py for new computation; its one-line terminal-descent correction is recorded in control/EVALUATOR-ERRATUM.md. All 92 imported refuted records remain authoritative at their exact scopes; use inputs/TRANSITIVE-REFUTED-CLAIMS.json for relevant identity checks. The final source paths are logical admission paths, not permission to write outside your scratch root. Do not read other workers, control-proposals, or previous-cycle raw outputs unless your packet explicitly names them.
