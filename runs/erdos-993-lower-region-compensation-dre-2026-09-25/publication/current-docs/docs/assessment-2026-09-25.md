## What failed and why it matters

Four precise local or algebraic mechanisms were refuted in this run: per-leaf unsigned deletion injectivity, same-current-rank weighted occupancy domination, the specified alternating-sign face-only retagging map, and local marked-indicator/addability nonpositive covariance. Their complete eligible aggregates stay nonpositive. These counterexamples eliminate proof shortcuts, not the target aggregate or Erdős #993.

First-descent transfer `CT_x`, flat addability `E<=(x+1)Q`, current-rank addability `E<=kQ`, and the actual budget `E-C<=kQ` are different statements. The first three are progressively weaker sufficient premises; the last is the target. A refutation of a current-rank covariance comparison is not a refutation of `CT_x`. Conversely, a proof of the incidence identity is not evidence that any of these inequalities holds.

The expanded deletion/two-for-one weighted Hall relation remains open. It is a sufficient transport mechanism, not equivalent to the scalar target. A positive full-layer deficit for a restricted relation or a deletion-only subproblem does not exhibit a deficient cut for the expanded relation. The original active-tag capacity must be used:

```
w_F(B) = #{v in F intersect B : (B-{v}) intersects (N_T(s_v)-{v})}.
```

Counting every favorable leaf present in `B` instead changes the problem. This error affected the original Cycle 6 heterogeneous flow experiment and the root-plus-arm sector discussion; original files are preserved and corrected evidence is separately attributed. Every future flow instrument should assert that total upper supply minus lower capacity equals the literal full aggregate before examining cuts or certificates.

An exhaustive census with zero eligible rows tests no sign instance of the guarded target. Cycle 6's labeled order-at-most-eight census has that limitation.
